Responsible Security Disclosure

Please do not publicly disclose security vulnerabilities until they have been investigated and, where appropriate, corrected.

Include reproduction steps, affected versions, proof-of-concept information, potential impact, and supporting evidence whenever possible.

Security reports should focus on reproducible technical impact. Spam, automated scanner output without validation, social-engineering requests, and reports that require destructive testing may not receive priority.

Remove passwords, private keys, access tokens, personal information, restricted species locations, and unrelated third-party data. When a report requires sensitive attachments, state that need in the initial message so a safer exchange method can be arranged.

Reports should identify the affected service, URL, repository, version, environment, prerequisites, reproduction steps, expected behavior, observed behavior, potential impact, and any proof-of-concept material needed to verify the issue.

Please do not publicly disclose a security vulnerability before it has been investigated and, where appropriate, corrected. Premature disclosure may place users, infrastructure, datasets, credentials, mirrors, or project services at additional risk.

Security Contact

Send responsible security disclosures to security@speciedex.org .

Include a concise summary in the subject line and clearly indicate whether the issue is believed to be actively exploitable.
