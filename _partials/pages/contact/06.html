Response Expectations

Response times depend on urgency, reproducibility, complexity, project workload, and whether specialist or institutional review is required.

General Questions

Normally reviewed within several business days, subject to project workload, availability, and whether the answer already exists in public documentation.

Technical Support

Responses depend on the completeness of version details, logs, reproduction steps, affected components, and whether the issue can be reproduced.

Security Reports

Reviewed as quickly as practical based on severity, exploitability, reproducibility, affected systems, and potential impact.

Research and Data Questions

May require provider review, source verification, taxonomic expertise, licensing analysis, or consultation with an external authority.

Partnership Requests

Response times vary with scope, technical requirements, licensing, institutional review, funding, and the complexity of the proposed collaboration.

Legal and Rights-Holder Notices

Reviewed according to the completeness of the notice, affected material, supporting evidence, jurisdiction, and requested action.
