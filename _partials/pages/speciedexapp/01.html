SpeciedexApp

The planned Android client for field observation, offline data collection, species learning, chain monitoring, public exploration, validation feedback, synchronization, and Bitcoin-funded bounty participation.

SpeciedexApp brings the Speciedex ecosystem into field, classroom, laboratory, community-science, conservation, and public-learning environments where a full desktop or server interface is impractical.

The application follows an offline-first design. Observations, evidence, drafts, signatures, downloaded taxonomic packages, maps, field guides, bounties, and validation rules remain useful when connectivity is unavailable, slow, intermittent, expensive, or untrusted.

Users may connect the app to a personal SpeciedexCore node, an institutional deployment, or trusted SpeciedexWeb and SpeciedexNet gateways. The selected path determines privacy, verification scope, available data, synchronization behavior, and administrative authority.

Identification tools and local models remain advisory. Suggested classifications do not become accepted species-chain records until the submission has passed the required validation, review, policy, and consensus process.

Permanent Protocol Boundary

SpeciedexApp permanently rejects Homo sapiens as a species blockchain, taxon record, observation target, specimen subject, bounty subject, biometric subject, genomic profile, or tracking target.

The application must not provide human biometric, facial, genomic, location-history, identity-correlation, surveillance, or person-profiling functions.
