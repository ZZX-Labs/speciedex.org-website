Safety

Permanent Human Exclusion and Field Safety

Permanent Human Exclusion

Homo sapiens is forbidden from being entered as a species blockchain, taxon record, observation target, specimen subject, bounty subject, biometric subject, genomic profile, or derived identity record.

The exclusion is constitutional to the protocol and is not an administrative preference that an operator may disable.

Boundary Enforcement

Validation rejects direct human records, aliases, disguised taxonomic entries, biometric datasets, genomic profiles, facial or gait records, identity graphs, and records whose practical purpose is tracking or profiling people.

Incidental references to human activity may appear only as necessary environmental context, provenance, authorship, conservation pressure, legal status, or interaction metadata attached to a permitted non-human record.

Sensitive Species

The app may blur, withhold, delay, or generalize coordinates for threatened, trafficked, nesting, breeding, or otherwise vulnerable species.

Responsible Collection

The application must not encourage trespass, harassment, illegal collection, habitat disturbance, unsafe travel, or contact with dangerous organisms.

Personal Safety

Field interfaces should support visible warnings, offline emergency notes, low-battery awareness, location-sharing controls, and project-specific safety guidance without turning observations into person tracking.

Media Privacy

Users should review images, audio, video, metadata, and backgrounds for unintended human identities, private property, sensitive locations, or confidential information before submission.
