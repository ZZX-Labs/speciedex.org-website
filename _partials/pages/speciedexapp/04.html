Connectivity

Offline-First Field Design

Local Queues

Observations, evidence, drafts, signatures, validation notes, and pending packages remain safely queued until a trusted synchronization path is available.

Regional Packages

Users may download selected taxa, maps, languages, guides, bounties, reference media, and validation rules before entering low-connectivity areas.

Resumable Media

Large images, audio, video, and field packages upload in verified, restartable chunks with stable identifiers and integrity checks.

Conflict-Safe Sync

Server changes do not silently overwrite local work; conflicting drafts, edits, assignments, and validation states remain explicit and reviewable.

Package Verification

Downloaded taxa, maps, guides, models, schemas, and bounty definitions retain checksums, signatures, versions, and source metadata.

Storage Controls

Users can inspect package size, media use, retention policy, offline scope, encryption state, and removable-cache options.
