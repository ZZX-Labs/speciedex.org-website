Trusted Contribution

Validation and Submission

On-Device Checks

The app checks required fields, formats, media integrity, impossible values, duplicate drafts, human exclusion, and selected taxonomic or geographic rules before submission.

Node Validation

A connected SpeciedexCore node performs authoritative protocol, evidence, provenance, policy, chain, object, and consensus checks.

Review Feedback

Users receive structured explanations for acceptance, rejection, conflict, requested evidence, expert review, or superseding revision.

Chain Receipt

Accepted work can return the species chain, block or entry identifier, commit state, propagation status, release state, and bounty result.

Draft Signing

Users can inspect the exact package, selected identity, evidence, destination node, and authorization scope before signing or handing off.

Revision Workflow

Rejected or conflicted work can be corrected without losing its local history, evidence lineage, prior review, or original identifiers.
