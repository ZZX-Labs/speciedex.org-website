Mobile Workflows

Application Modes

Observe

Capture permitted non-human species observations through guided, configurable, and project-aware forms.

Identify

Use taxonomic keys, comparisons, local indexes, media references, and optional model-assisted suggestions subject to validation.

Explore

Search and browse species, taxonomy, geography, ecological relationships, media, sources, and chain history.

Learn

Use lessons, glossaries, quizzes, field guides, conservation context, and downloaded local-species collections.

Watch

Follow validation feeds, selected species chains, network updates, field campaigns, alerts, and bounty events.

Contribute

Prepare signed submissions, corrections, translations, digitization, media, and bounty responses.

Synchronize

Queue work offline, verify local packages, resume transfers, and synchronize through a node or gateway.

Earn Bitcoin

Receive eligible bounty compensation through user-controlled Lightning or on-chain Bitcoin destinations.

Review

Inspect validation outcomes, requested evidence, conflicts, reviewer comments, chain receipts, and revision history.

Organize

Group observations, drafts, media, packages, maps, reading material, campaigns, and saved species lists.
