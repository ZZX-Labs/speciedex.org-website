Purpose

A Mobile Field and Learning Client

Field Collection

Create structured observations with time, location, environmental context, notes, measurements, media, methods, confidence, and offline evidence.

Forms can adapt to taxon, project, region, bounty, protocol version, or institutional workflow.

Public Learning

Read species profiles, taxonomic paths, maps, media, ecological relationships, conservation information, and educational guides.

Node Companion

Connect to a personal SpeciedexCore node over a trusted local or remote path for private synchronization, local verification, and advanced submission control.

Gateway Client

Use trusted SpeciedexWeb or SpeciedexNet gateways when operating a local node is impractical.

Campaign Client

Support expeditions, surveys, classrooms, conservation programs, institutional projects, and public field campaigns with shared packages and task definitions.

Validation Companion

Review structured feedback, missing evidence, conflicts, requested corrections, chain receipts, and bounty outcomes from connected nodes.
