Long-Term Vision

The creator’s long-term vision is not limited to launching a website or publishing a large database. It is to establish durable public infrastructure through which biodiversity knowledge can be searched, compared, cited, verified, preserved, mapped, analyzed, downloaded, and extended across generations.

Build

Create a comprehensive, scalable, searchable, and interoperable biological reference supported by open software and stable data models.

Preserve

Archive scientific information, provider histories, schemas, releases, documentation, media, and provenance in durable open formats.

Connect

Link species, classifications, publications, media, genetics, conservation, geography, specimens, observations, and institutional sources.

Verify

Publish checksums, signatures, manifests, source references, validation states, and reproducible processing information.

Distribute

Support independent mirrors, offline archives, low-bandwidth access, machine-readable APIs, applications, and portable datasets.

Share Stewardship

Develop governance, documentation, and contribution systems that allow the project to outgrow dependence on its creator.
