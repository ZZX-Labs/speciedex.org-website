From Creator-Led Project to Shared Infrastructure

Speciedex currently reflects the direction and sustained work of its creator, but its architecture is intended to support broader participation and independent continuity. The project should not require permanent personal control in order to remain functional, verifiable, or recoverable.

Transitioning toward shared infrastructure requires more than adding contributors. It requires stable specifications, public archives, documented roles, transparent release authority, independent mirrors, recoverable credentials, succession planning, and clear separation among scientific review, software maintenance, infrastructure operation, funding, and public communication.

The creator’s long-term responsibility is therefore partly to build the conditions under which Speciedex can continue responsibly without becoming dependent on the creator.
