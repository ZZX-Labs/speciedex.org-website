About the Creator and Stewardship of Speciedex

This page explains the role of the creator within Speciedex: how the project began, which responsibilities remain under independent stewardship, what principles guide technical and scientific decisions, and how the work is intended to become broader than any one individual.

The Creator page is not a second version of the global header, the root project overview, or the About page. Its purpose is narrower and more accountable. It documents the relationship between the person who initiated Speciedex and the open infrastructure that the project is intended to become.

Speciedex.org began as an independent research and engineering effort to create a permanent, freely accessible reference for taxonomy, biodiversity, scientific literature, conservation data, geographic information, genetic references, digital archives, and open technical infrastructure.

Although the project has a creator, its long-term value depends on open standards, documented processes, reproducible software, preserved provenance, independent review, distributed operation, and participation by researchers, institutions, developers, educators, archivists, and the public.
