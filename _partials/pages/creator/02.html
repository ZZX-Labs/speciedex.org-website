Independent Development

Speciedex was conceived and developed as an independent long-term project focused on scientific knowledge, software engineering, public documentation, data architecture, archival preservation, and open collaboration.

Independent development allows the project to establish its core architecture, standards, identity system, provider-ingestion model, archival procedures, and public-service principles without being constrained by the short-term priorities of a single grant, institution, vendor, or commercial product cycle.

Independence does not mean isolation. The project is designed to interoperate with taxonomic providers, museums, universities, governments, scientific repositories, conservation programs, libraries, archives, and open-source communities. It also depends on expert review and external correction whenever scientific or technical claims exceed the creator’s direct authority.

The goal is to establish a durable foundation that others can audit, reproduce, improve, mirror, integrate, and eventually help govern. A successful open infrastructure project should remain useful even when its original creator is not present.
