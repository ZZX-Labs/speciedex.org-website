Creator Responsibilities and Stewardship

Creating an open infrastructure project establishes responsibilities beyond authorship. The creator is responsible for clearly documenting the project’s origin, preserving attribution, separating personal decisions from scientific authority, publishing technical rationale, and making the system understandable to people who were not involved in its earliest development.

Stewardship also requires acknowledging limits. Taxonomic, conservation, genetic, legal, archival, security, and ethical questions may require review by specialists or institutions with domain-specific authority. The project should expose those boundaries rather than presenting creator judgment as universal expertise.

Document Decisions

Material architectural, scientific, and policy choices should include a written rationale and reviewable history.

Preserve Attribution

Sources, authors, providers, licenses, institutions, and contributors should remain visible throughout ingestion, transformation, export, and publication.

Invite Review

Software, schemas, records, releases, and project claims should remain open to correction and independent inspection.

Plan for Continuity

Documentation, archives, release procedures, schemas, and operating knowledge should survive changes in maintainers.
