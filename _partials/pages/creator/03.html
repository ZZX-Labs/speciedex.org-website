Guiding Philosophy

Knowledge about life on Earth should remain discoverable, attributable, verifiable, portable, and available to future generations. No single interface, institution, company, database, or funding program should be the only path through which important biodiversity records can be understood.

Speciedex therefore favors open documentation, transparent engineering, explicit provenance, reproducible transformations, stable identifiers, versioned schemas, independent archives, and interfaces that can be implemented by more than one operator.

The project also distinguishes openness from carelessness. Public access does not justify exposing sensitive locations, ignoring licenses, flattening scientific disagreement, erasing provider attribution, or presenting provisional information as settled fact.

The creator’s guiding responsibility is to make the system more inspectable and durable while preserving the scientific, institutional, ethical, and legal context of the material it connects.
