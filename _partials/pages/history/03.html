Development Timeline

The timeline presents the project’s current generalized history. Dates and descriptions are intentionally broad until original notes, prototypes, source records, documentation, and releases are formally cataloged and verified.

To make the history maintainable, the timeline is divided into four eras: conceptual formation, architectural development, distributed-system design, and public ecosystem construction. Each era preserves the original yearly milestones while adding context about how one stage led into the next.

2013–2015

Conceptual formation, information modeling, and the move from isolated records toward connected biological knowledge.

2016–2018

Technical evaluation, prototype planning, modular data structures, and implementation-oriented architecture.

2019–2021

Provenance, distributed access, independent operation, and separation into related system components.

2022–2026

Consolidation, renewed development, platform definition, public infrastructure, and active ecosystem construction.
