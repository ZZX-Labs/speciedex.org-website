Project Evolution

What Changed Over Time

Speciedex changed not only in scale but in kind. The project moved from a conceptual index, to a technical framework, to a family of interoperable platforms with distinct scientific, archival, public, and operational responsibilities.

Then

A Theoretical Index

The earliest concept focused on a comprehensive and connected index of species-related information.

Expansion

A Linked Knowledge Model

Species records became connected to taxonomy, literature, geography, observations, media, specimens, evidence, and history.

Development

A Technical Framework

The project developed schemas, provenance, search, interoperability, validation, preservation, and distributed access.

Architecture

A Modular Platform Family

Public interfaces, core software, exploration tools, network services, archives, analysis, maps, applications, and APIs became separate but coordinated responsibilities.

Now

An Open Ecosystem

Speciedex is being developed as an ecosystem of public resources, software, documentation, datasets, network services, and research tools.

Future

Durable Shared Infrastructure

The long-term goal is independently operable, verifiable, and preserved biodiversity infrastructure that can outlive any one maintainer.
