Continuity

A Project Designed for Revision

Scientific records change. Species are reclassified. Names are revised. New evidence appears. Old records are corrected. Providers change their schemas. Technologies become obsolete. Institutions reorganize or close.

Speciedex is therefore designed around revision rather than finality. Its history is not presented as a straight path from idea to finished product, but as an ongoing process of research, restructuring, implementation, review, preservation, and correction.

Historical documentation should preserve failed experiments, superseded schemas, renamed components, incompatible prototypes, abandoned directions, and the reasons later designs replaced them. These records help explain the present architecture and prevent earlier mistakes from being repeated.

The project history will become more detailed as original notes, prototypes, development records, release information, source commits, screenshots, archived websites, and published materials are prepared for formal inclusion.
