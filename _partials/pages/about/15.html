Acknowledgements

Speciedex is built upon decades of scientific research and the work of countless individuals and institutions.

Modern biodiversity science exists because generations of taxonomists, field biologists, ecologists, paleontologists, geneticists, conservationists, museum curators, collection managers, educators, illustrators, software developers, archivists, librarians, photographers, citizen scientists, and volunteers have documented the natural world.

Speciedex does not seek to replace their work or the institutions that preserve it. The project depends on published taxonomies, specimens, collections, literature, field observations, genetic repositories, conservation assessments, maps, media, standards, and open software produced by others.

The project’s responsibility is to preserve attribution, respect licensing, document transformations, retain provider identities, and make relationships among sources easier to inspect without claiming ownership of the knowledge they created.

We acknowledge every person, community, and organization that studies, preserves, teaches, funds, publishes, hosts, or openly shares knowledge about life on Earth.
