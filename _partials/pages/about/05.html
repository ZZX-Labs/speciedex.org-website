Project Architecture

Speciedex is one complete biodiversity infrastructure project supported by twelve specialized platforms with distinct responsibilities.

The architecture separates responsibilities without fragmenting the underlying knowledge. Every platform should remain interoperable through common identifiers, schemas, provenance records, validation rules, release practices, archive formats, and synchronization procedures.
