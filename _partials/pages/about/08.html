Project Scope

Speciedex is intended to document known life across recognized biological kingdoms and taxonomic ranks. It may include living species, extinct species, fossil taxa, microorganisms, viruses where relevant to provider coverage, disputed taxa, hybrids, subspecies, varieties, forms, provisional names, and historically significant classifications.

Related records may include ecosystems, habitats, biogeographic regions, observations, specimens, genetic sequences, field notes, ecological relationships, conservation assessments, media, and scientific publications when those records improve understanding or provenance.

Inclusion does not automatically imply scientific acceptance. Speciedex is designed to record disagreement, uncertainty, competing classifications, provider variation, and changes in scientific consensus rather than flattening them into a false appearance of certainty.

The project’s scope is broad, but publication remains bounded by licensing, ethics, conservation risk, privacy, cultural responsibility, data quality, technical feasibility, and the need for clear attribution.
