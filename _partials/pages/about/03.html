Vision

The long-term vision is a permanent digital index capable of representing every known species while remaining flexible enough to incorporate new discoveries, taxonomic revisions, extinct and fossil taxa, genetic evidence, conservation assessments, ecological data, geographic change, media archives, specimens, and scientific literature.

Speciedex is intended to remain useful across decades rather than individual release cycles. Every architectural decision should favor longevity, interoperability, clear provenance, reversible transformation, documented migration, reproducibility, and public accessibility.

The project should be usable at multiple scales: by an individual looking up a species, by a researcher comparing classifications, by a museum preserving collection links, by a government publishing biodiversity records, by a developer building new software, or by an institution operating an independent mirror and archive.

Success means more than comprehensive coverage. It means that records remain understandable, verifiable, portable, citable, historically traceable, and available through more than one interface or operator.
