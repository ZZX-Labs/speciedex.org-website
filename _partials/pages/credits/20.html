Final Acknowledgment

Thank You

The final acknowledgment returns credit to the individuals whose often invisible work makes biodiversity knowledge possible.

Thank you to every scientist who recorded an observation.

Thank you to every taxonomist who gave a species a name.

Thank you to every curator who preserved a specimen.

Thank you to every filmmaker who waited patiently for a moment that might last only seconds.

Thank you to every photographer who documented a place, behavior, organism, or ecosystem that others might never otherwise see.

Thank you to every conservationist who stood between a living species and extinction.

Thank you to every librarian, archivist, engineer, developer, reviewer, editor, translator, and volunteer who helped keep knowledge available.

Thank you to every child whose curiosity about an animal, plant, fungus, fossil, ocean, forest, desert, or distant world became the beginning of a lifelong search for understanding.

Speciedex stands on the shoulders of all of you.

Thank you.
