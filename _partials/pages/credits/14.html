Open Licensing

Creative Commons and Open Culture

Open licensing enables lawful education, research, preservation, and reuse while retaining attribution and respecting the terms chosen by creators and institutions.

We thank Creative Commons, open-access advocates, public-domain researchers, artists, photographers, institutions, and rights holders who make knowledge and media available under licenses that permit lawful education, preservation, research, and reuse.

Third-party materials remain subject to their respective licenses, attribution requirements, terms of use, copyright protections, and source-specific restrictions.
