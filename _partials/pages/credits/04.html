Institutions

Museums, Gardens, Universities, and Scientific Societies

Scientific institutions preserve specimens, maintain collections, train researchers, publish references, and provide the physical and institutional continuity upon which digital biodiversity systems depend.

We acknowledge the Smithsonian Institution, the Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History, the Natural History Museum in London, the American Museum of Natural History, the Field Museum, the California Academy of Sciences, the Australian Museum, Museums Victoria, Museum für Naturkunde Berlin, Naturalis Biodiversity Center, the Muséum national d’Histoire naturelle, the Royal Ontario Museum, the Canadian Museum of Nature, the National Museum of Natural History of India, the Bombay Natural History Society, the Zoological Survey of India, the Botanical Survey of India, the Indian Museum, the Wildlife Institute of India, and natural-history institutions throughout the world.

We acknowledge the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, the New York Botanical Garden, the Missouri Botanical Garden, the Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh, the Singapore Botanic Gardens, the Australian National Botanic Gardens, the Indian Botanic Garden, the National Tropical Botanical Garden, botanical gardens, arboreta, seed banks, fungal collections, and living scientific collections on every continent.

We acknowledge the Royal Society, the Linnean Society of London, the Zoological Society of London, the National Academy of Sciences, the American Association for the Advancement of Science, the Ecological Society of America, the Society for Conservation Biology, the International Society of Zoological Sciences, the International Association for Plant Taxonomy, the International Commission on Zoological Nomenclature, and the International Association for Biological Nomenclature.
