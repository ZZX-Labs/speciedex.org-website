Privacy, Encryption, and Open Communication

Privacy, encryption, and open communication tools protect people whose research, journalism, advocacy, identity, or safety depends on secure communication.

We thank the Tor Project, OpenVPN, Proton, GnuPG, OpenSSL, OpenSSH, WireGuard, Mozilla, Firefox, Signal, the Electronic Frontier Foundation, the Freedom of the Press Foundation, the Guardian Project, privacy researchers, security engineers, cryptographers, anonymity-network operators, relay operators, and digital-rights advocates.

Their work protects journalists, researchers, whistleblowers, dissidents, activists, survivors, vulnerable communities, and ordinary people who require privacy to communicate safely.
