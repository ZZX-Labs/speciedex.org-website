The People Behind the Data

Scientists, Naturalists, and Field Researchers

Every database and publication rests on the work of people who observe, collect, identify, compare, curate, digitize, review, and teach.

We thank taxonomists, zoologists, botanists, mycologists, microbiologists, ecologists, marine biologists, evolutionary biologists, geneticists, paleontologists, entomologists, ornithologists, mammalogists, herpetologists, ichthyologists, primatologists, conservation biologists, veterinarians, geographers, climatologists, oceanographers, foresters, environmental scientists, and every specialist who studies life.

We thank field assistants, specimen preparators, laboratory technicians, data-entry staff, translators, illustrators, scientific photographers, librarians, archivists, collection managers, database administrators, software engineers, peer reviewers, journal editors, educators, students, citizen scientists, and volunteers.

We thank the researchers who spent years identifying a single lineage, revising a difficult genus, describing a newly discovered organism, correcting an old error, digitizing a damaged specimen label, maintaining a neglected database, or documenting a species before it disappeared.
