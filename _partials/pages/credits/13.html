Conservation Technology

Wildbook

Wildbook represents an important example of conservation technology combining field observations, imagery, pattern recognition, collaboration, and individual-animal identification.

We extend special thanks to Wildbook, Wild Me, the developers of wildlife-identification systems, the researchers building computer-assisted individual-animal recognition, and the conservation programs applying machine learning to real-world species monitoring.

Wildbook demonstrated the extraordinary potential of combining field observations, photography, pattern recognition, citizen science, scientific collaboration, and open conservation technology.
