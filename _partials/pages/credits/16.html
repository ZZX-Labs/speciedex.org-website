Lightning

Lightning Network and LND

Lightning extends Bitcoin with payment-channel infrastructure designed for fast, low-cost transactions and interoperable application services.

We thank the authors, researchers, implementers, reviewers, node operators, routing researchers, wallet developers, infrastructure providers, and users of the Lightning Network.

We acknowledge Lightning Labs, LND, Core Lightning, Blockstream, ACINQ, Eclair, the Lightning Network specification contributors, BOLT authors and reviewers, and the wider community working to make Bitcoin payments faster, more private, and more broadly usable.
