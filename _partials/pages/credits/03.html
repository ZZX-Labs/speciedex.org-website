Scientific Foundations

Taxonomic, Biodiversity, and Species Databases

Speciedex acknowledges the scientific databases, taxonomic authorities, biodiversity networks, specimen repositories, conservation assessments, and reference projects that made modern digital biodiversity research possible.

Global Taxonomic Authorities

We acknowledge the Catalogue of Life, Species 2000, the Integrated Taxonomic Information System, the World Register of Marine Species, the Global Biodiversity Information Facility, the Encyclopedia of Life, Wikispecies, Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, the Tree of Life Web Project, the Open Tree of Life, the Global Names Architecture, the Global Names Index, the International Plant Names Index, the International Fossil Plant Names Index, Index Fungorum, MycoBank, ZooBank, Tropicos, World Flora Online, Plants of the World Online, and the World Checklist of Vascular Plants.

Conservation Assessment Systems

We acknowledge the International Union for Conservation of Nature, the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species, the IUCN Green Status of Species, the IUCN Green List of Protected and Conserved Areas, BirdLife International, NatureServe, the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora, the Convention on Biological Diversity, the Ramsar Convention, the Convention on Migratory Species, Protected Planet, and the World Database on Protected Areas.

Marine and Aquatic Databases

We acknowledge the World Register of Marine Species, the Ocean Biodiversity Information System, FishBase, SeaLifeBase, AlgaeBase, the World Porifera Database, the World Polychaeta Database, the World Register of Deep-Sea Species, the World Register of Introduced Marine Species, the Marine Species Identification Portal, AquaMaps, Reef Life Survey, ReefBase, the Census of Marine Life, the Barcode of Life Data System, and the Global Ocean Observing System.

Plants, Fungi, and Botanical Data

We acknowledge the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, Plants of the World Online, the International Plant Names Index, World Flora Online, the Missouri Botanical Garden, Tropicos, the New York Botanical Garden, the Global Plants Initiative, Index Fungorum, MycoBank, the USDA PLANTS Database, Flora of North America, Flora of China, Euro+Med PlantBase, the African Plant Database, the Australian Plant Census, the Australian Plant Name Index, the International Association for Plant Taxonomy, and the herbaria and botanical collections that preserve the physical record of plant diversity.

Vertebrate Databases

We acknowledge the Mammal Diversity Database, Mammal Species of the World, the IOC World Bird List, Avibase, eBird, Birds of the World, BirdLife International, AmphibiaWeb, Amphibian Species of the World, AmphibiaChina, the Reptile Database, FishBase, SeaLifeBase, Eschmeyer’s Catalog of Fishes, California Academy of Sciences Ichthyology, the Global Shark and Ray Initiative, and the many specialist groups that maintain authoritative vertebrate taxonomies.

Invertebrate Databases

We acknowledge AntWeb, AntCat, the Global Ant Biodiversity Informatics project, the Orthoptera Species File, the Mantodea Species File, the Phasmida Species File, the Cockroach Species File, the Plecoptera Species File, the Neuropterida Species of the World, the Lepidoptera and Other Life Forms database, Butterflies and Moths of North America, OdonataCentral, MolluscaBase, Aphia, the World Spider Catalog, the World Arachnida Catalog, the Global Register of Introduced and Invasive Species, and the countless specialist taxonomic catalogues maintained by entomologists and invertebrate zoologists.

Microbiology and Genetics

We acknowledge the National Center for Biotechnology Information, NCBI Taxonomy, GenBank, the European Nucleotide Archive, the DNA Data Bank of Japan, UniProt, the Protein Data Bank, the Barcode of Life Data System, the International Barcode of Life Consortium, SILVA, Ribosomal Database Project, LPSN, BacDive, MycoBank, the Genome Taxonomy Database, Ensembl, and the researchers who make molecular and genomic data available for comparative science.

Fossils and Paleontology

We acknowledge the Paleobiology Database, Fossilworks, the International Fossil Plant Names Index, the NOW Database of Fossil Mammals, the Neotoma Paleoecology Database, PaleoBioDB contributors, museum paleontology departments, fossil collections, geological surveys, and the scientists reconstructing the history of life from the geological record.

Specimens and Natural-History Collections

We acknowledge the Integrated Digitized Biocollections network, the Biodiversity Heritage Library, the Distributed System of Scientific Collections, the Global Registry of Scientific Collections, natural-history museums, university collections, herbaria, seed banks, tissue banks, zoological collections, microbial culture collections, and every curator and collections manager preserving irreplaceable scientific material.

Citizen Science

We acknowledge iNaturalist, eBird, Observation.org, Project Noah, Nature’s Notebook, Reef Life Survey, Zooniverse, BugGuide, HerpMapper, Mushroom Observer, PlantNet, Seek, Merlin, and the millions of citizen scientists whose observations strengthen the global record of biodiversity.
