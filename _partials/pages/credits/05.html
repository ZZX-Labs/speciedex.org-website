Conservation Organizations and Protected-Life Networks

Conservation organizations, protected-area networks, local communities, field teams, and wildlife responders transform biodiversity knowledge into direct protection and stewardship.

We thank the Schmidt Ocean Institute, Mission Blue, the Cousteau Society, Ocean Conservancy, Oceana, the Monterey Bay Aquarium, the Marine Conservation Society, the Coral Reef Alliance, Reef Check, Sea Shepherd, the Ocean Cleanup, the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, the Scripps Institution of Oceanography, MBARI, NOAA Ocean Exploration, and the crews, pilots, divers, engineers, and scientists who explore and protect the ocean.

Wildlife Rescue and Rehabilitation

We recognize wildlife hospitals, rescue centers, rehabilitation programs, sanctuaries, veterinary teams, anti-poaching units, rangers, community conservation groups, and emergency responders who protect wildlife directly, often under dangerous and underfunded conditions.

Indigenous and Local Knowledge

We recognize Indigenous peoples, forest communities, fishing communities, pastoral communities, farmers, traditional ecological knowledge holders, local guides, trackers, and community guardians whose knowledge and stewardship have protected species and ecosystems across generations.
