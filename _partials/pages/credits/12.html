Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence

Machine-learning and artificial-intelligence tools can assist organization, classification, interface development, and software work, but human direction and scientific validation remain essential.

We acknowledge TensorFlow, Google Brain, Keras, PyTorch, the Linux Foundation, scikit-learn, JAX, ONNX, OpenCV, Hugging Face, OpenAI, ChatGPT, Stability AI, Stable Diffusion, the broader open-model community, academic AI laboratories, independent researchers, dataset curators, model evaluators, and engineers advancing machine learning.

Artificial-intelligence systems have assisted with research organization, software drafting, testing, documentation, classification concepts, interface development, and exploratory design within the wider Speciedex development process.

Speciedex remains a human-directed project. Editorial judgment, scientific validation, project governance, final technical decisions, and responsibility for published material remain with the project creator and maintainers.
