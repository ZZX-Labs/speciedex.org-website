Credits and Acknowledgments

A public record of the people, institutions, databases, scientific communities, conservation organizations, filmmakers, archives, open-source projects, Bitcoin contributors, and technical systems whose work informed, inspired, enabled, or supported Speciedex.

The Credits page exists to document intellectual, scientific, cultural, technical, and infrastructural lineage. Speciedex did not emerge in isolation. It depends on generations of taxonomy, museum and collection work, biodiversity databases, field research, conservation, documentary media, software engineering, open standards, public archives, cryptography, distributed systems, and volunteer knowledge communities.

This page does not claim formal affiliation with every person or organization named below. Inclusion recognizes influence, inspiration, public contribution, scientific contribution, technical contribution, preservation work, or participation in the wider ecosystem from which Speciedex draws.

Credit is separated from ownership. Organization names, trademarks, source materials, publications, media, datasets, software, and database records remain governed by their respective owners, creators, licenses, attribution requirements, and source-specific restrictions.

The acknowledgments are organized by major field so readers can understand the foundations beneath the project: creator credit, scientific databases, institutions, conservation, documentary media, Earth observation, archives, open knowledge, software, artificial intelligence, Bitcoin, privacy technology, field science, dedication, and legal attribution.
