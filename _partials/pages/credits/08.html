Earth and Space

Exploration, Mapping, Climate, and Earth Observation

Earth-observation, mapping, oceanographic, climate, and space programs provide the geographic and environmental context required to understand species and ecosystems.

We thank NASA, the European Space Agency, NOAA, the United States Geological Survey, the National Science Foundation, the National Geographic Society, the Schmidt Ocean Institute, the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, the Scripps Institution of Oceanography, the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute, the National Oceanography Centre, the Indian Space Research Organisation, the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, and the Earth-observation programs that make planetary data available to researchers and the public.

We acknowledge OpenStreetMap, the OpenStreetMap Foundation, Natural Earth, GeoNames, What3Words, Mapillary, geospatial open-data communities, national mapping agencies, hydrographic offices, remote-sensing programs, and cartographers whose work helps connect species with place.
