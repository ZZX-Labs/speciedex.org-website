Preservation

Libraries, Archives, and Open Knowledge

Libraries, archives, repositories, and open-scholarship systems preserve the documents, metadata, and historical record needed for reproducible research.

Public Archives

We thank the Internet Archive, the Wayback Machine, Project Gutenberg, the Biodiversity Heritage Library, HathiTrust, Europeana, the Digital Public Library of America, the Library of Congress, the British Library, national libraries, university libraries, museum libraries, and public archives preserving scientific and cultural knowledge.

Open Scholarship

We acknowledge arXiv, bioRxiv, medRxiv, Zenodo, PubMed, PubMed Central, Europe PMC, Crossref, ORCID, the Directory of Open Access Journals, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, CORE, Unpaywall, institutional repositories, open-access journals, and researchers who make their work publicly available.

Community Preservation

We acknowledge Anna’s Archive, Library Genesis, community-maintained archives, digital librarians, preservation volunteers, metadata maintainers, and those who work to prevent knowledge from disappearing through neglect, censorship, technical failure, or commercial abandonment.

Wikimedia

We thank the Wikimedia Foundation, Wikipedia, Wikispecies, Wikimedia Commons, Wikidata, Wikibooks, Wikisource, Wikiversity, Wikivoyage, and the global volunteer community whose work demonstrates the extraordinary public value of collaborative knowledge.
