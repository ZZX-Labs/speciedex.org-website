SpeciedexTerminal

The command-line and terminal-based interface for searching, validating, synchronizing, exporting, diagnosing, visualizing, and automating the Speciedex ecosystem.

SpeciedexTerminal provides direct access to SpeciedexCore services through an interactive console, command-line client, scripted commands, structured output, live event feeds, and operational diagnostics.

It is intended for researchers, developers, system administrators, taxonomic data curators, archivists, institutions, field teams, and automated workflows that require precise control beyond a general browser interface.

The terminal can operate against a local SpeciedexCore node, an institutional deployment, a trusted SpeciedexNet gateway, a read-only public service, or an authenticated administrative endpoint with explicitly bounded permissions.

Permanent Protocol Boundary

SpeciedexTerminal must not expose commands for creating, searching, tracking, profiling, mapping, or analyzing Homo sapiens as a Speciedex species record.

Human biometric datasets, human genomic profiles, identity graphs, and person-tracking functions remain prohibited at the protocol level.

Search Precisely

Query species, taxonomy, providers, chains, records, observations, maps, references, media, and provenance.

Operate Nodes

Inspect synchronization, peers, storage, indexes, providers, releases, archives, validation, and system health.

Automate Workflows

Use scripts, pipelines, structured output, batch jobs, deterministic replay, and machine-readable exit states.

Visualize Activity

Follow live validation, chain history, network events, maps, timelines, matrices, graphs, and statistical views.
