Trust Boundaries

Security and Constitutional Rules

Permanent Human Exclusion

Homo sapiens is forbidden as a species chain, taxon record, observation target, specimen subject, bounty subject, biometric subject, genomic profile, or tracking target.

Least Authority

Read, query, submit, validate, publish, administer, archive, and wallet functions use separate permissions and credentials.

Secret Protection

Private keys, seed phrases, wallet passwords, API tokens, macaroons, signing keys, and administrative credentials must never be printed into ordinary terminal output or logs.

Safe Defaults

Destructive, privileged, publication, payout, and migration commands require explicit targets, validation, and bounded authority.

Output Redaction

Sensitive species locations, private participant details, credentials, restricted objects, and harmful payloads remain redacted.

Quarantined Failure

Invalid objects, corrupt indexes, incompatible schemas, failed imports, and suspicious peer data cannot replace verified local state.

Never Paste Wallet or Administrative Secrets

Speciedex support does not require a Bitcoin seed phrase, Lightning seed, private key, wallet password, administrative token, recovery backup, or remote desktop session.
