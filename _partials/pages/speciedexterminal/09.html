Deployment

Integration and Access Paths

Local SpeciedexCore

Use a locally operated node for independent verification, privacy, offline analysis, and direct storage access.

Institutional Node

Connect to museum, university, laboratory, archive, conservation, or agency infrastructure with controlled collections.

SpeciedexNet Gateway

Use an authenticated or public gateway when complete local node operation is impractical.

SpeciedexAPI

Invoke versioned machine-readable services and preserve request, response, schema, pagination, and release metadata.

SpeciedexArchives

Verify and restore releases, provider snapshots, checksums, manifests, documentation, and recovery packages.

SpeciedexExplorer

Hand off selected records, queries, maps, timelines, and analysis state to a graphical research interface.

SpeciedexMaps

Open geospatial results, ranges, observations, habitats, protected areas, uncertainty, and historical layers.

External Tools

Integrate with Python, shell tools, databases, notebooks, GIS systems, reports, schedulers, and institutional pipelines.
