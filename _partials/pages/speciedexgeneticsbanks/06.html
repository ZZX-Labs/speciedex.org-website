Constitutional and Legal Boundaries

Ethics, Law, and Permanent Human Exclusion

Permanent Human Exclusion

Homo sapiens genomes, sequences, genotypes, phenotypes, clinical data, ancestry profiles, biometric records, identity-linked samples, and derived profiles are forbidden.

The exclusion is constitutional to the Speciedex protocol and cannot be disabled by a repository adapter, node operator, administrator, API client, or research workflow.

Environmental Sample Controls

Metagenomic, environmental DNA, microbiome, food, water, soil, air, and mixed samples require screening and policy controls preventing human genetic material from becoming an indexed or recoverable profile.

Wildlife and Conservation Law

Records may be restricted by endangered-species law, wildlife trade controls, permits, access agreements, protected-area rules, collection law, or conservation policy.

Genetic Resource Governance

Preserve applicable access, benefit-sharing, material transfer, repository, institutional, national, and international restrictions where documented.

Sensitive Localities

Voucher and sample coordinates may be generalized, delayed, withheld, or restricted when disclosure could facilitate collection, trafficking, disturbance, or habitat damage.

No Clinical or Identity Use

The platform is not a medical database, ancestry service, forensic identity system, biometric registry, or human surveillance infrastructure.

Repository Availability Does Not Equal Permission

A publicly resolvable accession does not automatically authorize unrestricted redistribution, commercial use, sample access, collection, or removal of legal and ethical restrictions.
