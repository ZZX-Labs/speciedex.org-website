Interoperability

Accessions, Specimens, and Repository Links

Persistent Accessions

Preserve repository accession, version, namespace, release, status, retrieval date, replacement record, and external resolver.

Voucher Specimens

Connect genetic records to museum, herbarium, tissue-bank, culture-collection, or institutional voucher identifiers.

Collection Events

Link permitted locality, date, collector role, sampling method, habitat, host, substrate, depth, and environmental context.

Source Taxon Concepts

Retain the repository's original organism name, taxon ID, strain, isolate, lineage, and classification at retrieval time.

Publication Links

Connect accessions to papers, datasets, methods, supplementary files, corrections, and repository submissions.

Cross-Repository Mapping

Link corresponding records across nucleotide, protein, structure, barcode, genome, specimen, and taxonomic repositories.
