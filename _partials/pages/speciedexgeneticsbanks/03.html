Genomic, mitochondrial, chloroplast, ribosomal, marker, locus, transcript, and other permitted sequence references.

Gene identifiers, names, symbols, products, transcripts, coordinates, orthology, annotation state, and source databases.

Protein accessions, sequences, names, functions, structures, domains, evidence, annotations, and repository links.

Barcode records, marker regions, specimen vouchers, collection metadata, identification confidence, and project links.

Phylogenetic Data

Alignments, trees, models, clades, support values, source matrices, software, parameters, and publications.

Structural Biology

Macromolecular structures, methods, chains, ligands, resolutions, validation reports, and source accessions.
