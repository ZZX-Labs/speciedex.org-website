Trust

Validation and Data Quality

Identifier Validation

Check accession syntax, namespace, version, repository, resolver state, replacement links, and duplicate references.

Taxonomic Validation

Compare repository labels with accepted names, synonyms, strains, isolates, voucher identifications, and chain state.

Specimen Validation

Verify voucher identifiers, collection codes, repository records, sample relationships, and publication evidence.

Sequence Metadata Validation

Check molecule type, length, topology, completeness, assembly level, annotation, genetic code, and release metadata.

Contamination Flags

Preserve repository warnings, screening results, chimeric records, uncertain origin, contamination reports, and exclusions.

Provenance Validation

Verify source, retrieval date, release, transformation, checksum, license, attribution, and mapping history.

Conflict Analysis

Expose conflicting species assignments, vouchers, annotations, repository mappings, publications, and revisions.

Policy Validation

Reject human genetic records, prohibited samples, unsupported licenses, restricted personal data, and protocol-invalid content.
