Purpose

A Genetic Reference Layer for Non-Human Life

Repository Interoperability

Normalize identifiers and metadata from established sequence, protein, genome, barcode, specimen, culture, and biological collection repositories without replacing them.

Taxonomic Reconciliation

Connect repository taxon labels, historical names, strains, isolates, synonyms, and source concepts to versioned Speciedex taxonomic identities.

Specimen Context

Link sequences to voucher specimens, tissue samples, cultures, collection events, repositories, geographic context, and scientific publications where permitted.

Chain-Aware History

Preserve when a genetic reference was linked, revised, disputed, superseded, withdrawn, or reclassified within a species chain.

Scientific Discovery

Support comparative biology, taxonomy, phylogeny, conservation genetics, microbial classification, environmental research, and reproducible data discovery.

Reference, Not Ownership

External records remain controlled by their repositories, submitters, licenses, access policies, and applicable law.
