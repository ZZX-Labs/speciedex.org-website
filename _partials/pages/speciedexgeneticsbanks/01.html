SpeciedexGeneticBank

The non-human genetic-data indexing and reference platform that connects verified species records with genomes, genes, sequences, specimens, markers, accessions, publications, methods, and established biological repositories.

SpeciedexGeneticBank does not replace GenBank, the European Nucleotide Archive, the DNA Data Bank of Japan, UniProt, the Protein Data Bank, BOLD, SILVA, GTDB, institutional biobanks, culture collections, tissue repositories, museums, herbaria, or other established scientific archives.

It provides a normalized interoperability layer that links authorized non-human genetic references to canonical Speciedex taxa, source taxonomic concepts, voucher specimens, collection records, accession identifiers, repository releases, scientific literature, evidence objects, and applicable ethical or legal restrictions.

Genetic records remain attributable to their original repositories. Speciedex preserves references, metadata, checksums, provenance, validation state, taxonomic mappings, chain relationships, and access conditions rather than claiming ownership of external data.

Permanent Human-Genetics Boundary

Homo sapiens sequences, genomes, genotypes, biometric profiles, ancestry profiles, clinical records, identity-linked samples, and person-tracking datasets are permanently excluded.

Mixed or environmental samples require controls that prevent human genetic material from becoming an indexed Speciedex record or recoverable human profile.

Link Taxa and Accessions

Connect repository records to accepted species identities, synonyms, source concepts, specimens, and chain state.

Preserve Provenance

Retain repository, accession, release, retrieval date, method, specimen, publication, license, and transformation history.

Validate Relationships

Check taxonomy, sequence metadata, voucher links, identifiers, contamination flags, licenses, and policy boundaries.

Support Reproducibility

Bind analyses and exports to repository versions, Speciedex releases, schemas, queries, and immutable manifests.
