Thirteen Integrated Platforms

The Speciedex Ecosystem

Speciedex is the complete concept and shared system. Each of its twelve specialized components is designed to operate independently where necessary while remaining interoperable with the wider platform. Shared identifiers, schemas, metadata policies, archival formats, verification procedures, and synchronization protocols allow data to move consistently between the public website, local software, distributed nodes, research databases, libraries, analytical systems, mapping services, archival repositories, and third-party applications.

Platform 01

Speciedex

The complete Speciedex concept in one unified biodiversity system, including the public species index and every connected platform.

Speciedex maintains the shared identity, canonical taxonomic records, scientific names, common names, rank relationships, authorities, synonyms, geographic distributions, conservation information, media references, source provenance, persistent identifiers, and operating principles used across the ecosystem.

Platform 02

SpeciedexCore

The foundational software libraries, data models, normalization engine, indexing systems, validation framework, and reference implementation used throughout Speciedex.

SpeciedexCore processes provider records, resolves synonyms and authorities, applies taxonomic policies, verifies schemas, records provenance, detects conflicts, generates canonical identifiers, and produces normalized datasets for every supported interface.

The interactive discovery, visualization, and research interface for navigating the complete Speciedex dataset.

Researchers and visitors can explore taxonomic trees, lineage relationships, provider records, classifications, ecological associations, conservation status, genetic references, historical revisions, linked literature, and connected geographic information.

Platform 04

SpeciedexNet

The distributed network layer responsible for dataset synchronization, mirrors, peer services, replication, verification, and infrastructure interoperability.

SpeciedexNet enables institutions and independent operators to maintain complete or partial mirrors, exchange signed manifests, verify archive integrity, distribute updates, and preserve public access without depending upon a single centralized host.

The genetic-data indexing and reference platform for connecting species records with genomes, genes, sequences, specimens, markers, and biological repositories.
