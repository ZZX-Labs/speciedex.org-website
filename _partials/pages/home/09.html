Bitcoin Ecosystem

Open monetary, verification, timestamping, and distributed-system standards that inform the Bitcoin-native design of Speciedex.

Speciedex uses Bitcoin as an open settlement and funding layer and draws broader architectural lessons from independently verifiable software, public protocols, signed artifacts, reproducible operation, and resistance to centralized control. Bitcoin mechanisms may support donations, contributor payments, archival sponsorship, node operation, cryptographic commitments, and timestamped dataset publication without replacing scientific citation or ordinary archival practice.
