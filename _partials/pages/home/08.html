Resources

Documentation, scientific literature, datasets, schemas, downloads, APIs, technical specifications, and public development resources.

Speciedex resources are intended to support both human reading and reproducible technical work. Manuals explain how the project operates; research and library records preserve scientific context; schemas and APIs support software integration; downloads and archives provide verifiable artifacts; and public repositories expose the implementation, issue history, and contribution process.
