Speciedex Ecosystem

Thirteen coordinated platforms provide the public index, core data systems, research interfaces, distributed services, archives, analysis, mapping, applications, and machine-readable access.

Speciedex is the complete concept in one. Twelve specialized platforms support that unified project while sharing identifiers, schemas, provenance records, validation policies, archives, release practices, and synchronization methods. Each platform can be entered directly from this dashboard, but none is intended to become an isolated copy of the underlying biodiversity knowledge.
