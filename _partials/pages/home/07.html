Roadmap

Current engineering priorities, near-term integration work, and long-range milestones for the complete thirteen-platform ecosystem.

The roadmap is organized by execution horizon rather than by isolated product. Work completed in SpeciedexCore, provider ingestion, schemas, archives, maps, analysis, and APIs becomes shared infrastructure for the public index and every user-facing interface. Priorities may change as scientific requirements, provider availability, validation results, and operational needs evolve.

Now

  • Core database architecture
  • Static website and page-component framework
  • Documentation and provider-record structure
  • Complete multi-provider species ingestion
  • Canonical taxonomy reconciliation and validation
  • Public search, browsing, and record-comparison workflows
  • Release, checksum, and archive publication pipeline

Next

  • Geographic distribution and protected-area mapping
  • SpeciedexLibrary literature and citation indexing
  • SpeciedexAnalysis comparison and quality-control tools
  • Media and specimen-reference integration
  • Genetic accession and repository interoperability
  • Versioned SpeciedexAPI services and schemas
  • Desktop, mobile, field, and offline application access

Future

  • Distributed institutional and independent mirrors
  • Signed synchronization and reproducible archive verification
  • Bitcoin and Lightning funding and contributor services
  • Citizen-science observation and review workflows
  • University, museum, repository, and government partnerships
  • Global biodiversity atlas and temporal-change analysis
  • Long-duration preservation across open archival formats
