Community

Ways for individuals, institutions, researchers, maintainers, educators, designers, archivists, and infrastructure operators to participate.

Building durable biodiversity infrastructure requires scientific expertise, engineering, documentation, design, translation, archival work, institutional cooperation, funding, and independent operation. Participation can range from a single correction or translation to maintaining a mirror, integrating a research collection, contributing software, reviewing taxonomy, or sponsoring preservation.
