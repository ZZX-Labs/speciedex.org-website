Public Service

Privacy, Accessibility, and Performance

No Human Tracking

The site provides no human species records, biometric lookup, person search, human location history, or human genomic profiles.

Sensitive Species Controls

Coordinates or details that could facilitate poaching, collection, harassment, or habitat damage may be generalized, delayed, or withheld.

Accessible Interface

Semantic structure, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, labels, reduced-motion support, captions, transcripts, and responsive layouts guide implementation.

Progressive Enhancement

Core reading and navigation remain functional with minimal JavaScript; maps, playback, feeds, and analysis enhance the base document.

Caching and Resilience

Static assets, public records, and safe query results may be cached while freshness indicators prevent stale data from appearing current.

Rate and Abuse Controls

Public gateways protect resources through reasonable limits, request validation, quotas, and abuse detection without altering the open dataset.

Privacy-Minimized Logging

Operational logs should avoid unnecessary personal data, redact secrets, and retain only what is needed for security and reliability.

Performance Budgets

Pages should limit unnecessary scripts, media, network requests, and blocking resources so core information remains usable on constrained connections.
