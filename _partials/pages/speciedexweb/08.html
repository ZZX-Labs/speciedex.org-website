Safety Boundary

Permanent Human Exclusion

Permanent Human Exclusion

Homo sapiens is forbidden from being entered as a species blockchain, taxon record, observation target, specimen subject, bounty subject, biometric subject, genomic profile, or derived identity record.

The exclusion is constitutional to the protocol and is not an administrative preference that a gateway operator may disable.

Boundary Enforcement

Validation rejects direct human records, aliases, disguised taxonomic entries, biometric datasets, genomic profiles, facial or gait records, identity graphs, and records whose practical purpose is tracking or profiling people.

Incidental references to human activity may appear only as necessary environmental context, provenance, authorship, conservation pressure, legal status, or interaction metadata attached to a permitted non-human record.

Interface Enforcement

Search suggestions, filters, maps, profile routes, APIs, forms, exports, analytics, and bounty workflows must not create a back door for human indexing.

Privacy Purpose

The boundary prevents SpeciedexWeb from becoming a person-search, biometric lookup, population surveillance, social-scoring, or genomic profiling service.
