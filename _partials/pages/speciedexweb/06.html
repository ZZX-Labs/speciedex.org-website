The Validation Ticker

Incoming

Shows signed submissions, provider imports, field packages, and queued objects entering validation.

Checking

Shows schema, taxonomy, evidence, geography, provenance, duplication, policy, signature, and object checks.

Accepted

Shows entries committed to a species chain, indexed locally, and propagated to peers.

Rejected

Shows non-sensitive rejection reasons without exposing credentials, personal data, restricted locations, or harmful payloads.

Conflicted

Shows records requiring comparison, challenge, expert review, supersession, or unresolved scientific representation.

Revised

Shows superseding, correcting, qualifying, disputing, or retracting records.

Bounty

Shows public task state from publication through submission, validation, approval, and Bitcoin settlement.

Network

Shows gateway health, chain lag, peer reachability, object availability, provider status, and feed continuity.

Archived

Shows completed releases, snapshots, checksums, manifests, and historical packages entering long-term preservation.

Recovered

Shows repaired indexes, resumed transfers, restored objects, completed migrations, and cleared synchronization faults.
