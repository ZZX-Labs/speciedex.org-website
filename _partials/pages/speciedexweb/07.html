Submission and Bounty Access

Web Submissions

Supported users may prepare structured observations, corrections, media, references, translations, or bounty responses through guided forms and upload workflows.

Signed Handoff

Where stronger local signing, offline evidence handling, or private-key isolation is required, the website can hand work to SpeciedexCore or SpeciedexApp.

Bitcoin Rewards

Eligible accepted work may be compensated in Bitcoin on-chain or through the Lightning Network under published bounty terms.

No Custodial Requirement

The website need not hold user funds. Payout addresses or Lightning invoices can be handled through documented minimal-disclosure workflows.

Draft and Review

Users can save local or account-scoped drafts, validate required fields, inspect evidence requirements, and review the exact submission package before signing.

Submission Status

Accepted, rejected, conflicted, challenged, revised, and bounty-settlement states can be followed through stable submission identifiers.
