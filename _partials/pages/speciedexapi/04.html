Service Surface

Primary API Services

Species

Retrieve current species profiles, stable identifiers, scientific names, traits, ecology, conservation, media, references, and chain metadata.

Taxonomy

Traverse classifications, ranks, parent and child taxa, synonyms, authorities, concepts, competing taxonomies, splits, mergers, and historical placements.

Blockchains

Inspect species-chain headers, blocks, entries, canonical tips, checkpoints, signatures, historical state, and validation outcomes.

Observations

Query accepted occurrence records by species, time, geography, evidence, observer type, source, confidence, review state, and chain position.

Geography

Access ranges, regions, habitats, protected areas, uncertainty, environmental context, elevation, depth, migration, and permitted map layers.

Relationships

Query predation, pollination, host, parasite, symbiotic, competitive, habitat, food-web, and other ecological relationships.

Media and Objects

Resolve content-addressed media metadata, checksums, formats, dimensions, licenses, attribution, derivatives, and species-chain references.

Research

Retrieve publications, datasets, claims, methods, citations, specimen references, identifiers, and linked scientific evidence.

Conservation

Query assessments, threats, population trends, legal protection, recovery activity, evidence age, authorities, and restricted-data handling.

Validation

Validate schemas, taxonomy, provenance, geography, evidence, policy, duplication, object availability, and proposed chain entries.

Network

Inspect node status, chain synchronization, peer health, release compatibility, object availability, feed state, and gateway metadata.

Bounties

Browse public data tasks, requirements, reward terms, evidence standards, review status, completion state, and Bitcoin settlement metadata.
