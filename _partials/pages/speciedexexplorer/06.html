Scientific Work

Analysis Tools

Distribution Analysis

Measure occurrence density, temporal change, uncertainty, sampling effort, geographic gaps, and introduced or declining range.

Trait Analysis

Compare measurements, life histories, diagnostic traits, developmental stages, variation, and source confidence.

Ecological Networks

Inspect interaction networks and calculate connectedness, dependency, host breadth, or ecosystem roles.

Conservation Analysis

Compare assessments, threats, protection, observation frequency, habitat loss, and evidence age.

Provider Comparison

Compare how sources classify, describe, identify, or assess the same species and expose unresolved differences.

Data Quality

Identify missing fields, stale claims, weak evidence, improbable values, duplicates, and candidate bounty tasks.

Temporal Analysis

Track how classifications, observations, ranges, threats, and evidence changed across dates, releases, and chain heights.

Provenance Analysis

Measure source coverage, transformation depth, reviewer activity, provider dependence, and unsupported assertions.

Genetic Cross-Reference

Compare permitted genetic references, specimens, accessions, markers, publications, and taxonomic concepts.

Export and Reporting

Generate reproducible tables, maps, figures, reports, notebooks, and machine-readable result packages.
