Evidence, Provenance, and Citation

Every View Is Traceable

A displayed claim can resolve to its species chain, block or entry, source record, object hash, validator state, schema version, and later revisions.

Citation Builder

Generate stable citations for current records, historical chain states, filtered queries, maps, data exports, media objects, and analytical notebooks.

Exportable Results

Export permitted views as JSON, JSON Lines, CSV, TSV, GeoJSON, text reports, images, notebooks, or reproducible query definitions.

Source and License Visibility

Provider identity, original record identifier, retrieval date, transformation history, licensing, attribution, and usage restrictions remain visible where applicable.

Verification Status

Explorer shows whether a record was verified locally, received from a trusted remote source, partially synchronized, historically archived, or derived from an unverified view.

No Human Dataset

Explorer does not provide human species pages, human observation search, biometric analysis, genomic profiles, or tracking functions because the protocol forbids such records.
