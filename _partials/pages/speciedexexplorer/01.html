SpeciedexExplorer

The advanced analytical and visual discovery client for searching, mapping, comparing, replaying, visualizing, and studying the verified species chains and linked scientific records of the Speciedex ecosystem.

SpeciedexExplorer transforms verified chain data into species profiles, maps, timelines, taxonomic trees, relationship graphs, comparison workspaces, provenance trails, media views, research notebooks, and reproducible analytical outputs.

Explorer can operate against a local SpeciedexCore node, an institutional deployment, a trusted SpeciedexNet gateway, or the public SpeciedexWeb service. The selected access path determines available data, privacy, performance, offline capability, and the degree of independent verification.

The interface preserves scientific disagreement rather than hiding it. Canonical summaries remain distinguishable from source assertions, disputed records, historical classifications, confidence states, review outcomes, and unresolved conflicts.

Explorer never provides a Homo sapiens species page, observation search, biometric analysis, genomic profile, or human tracking function because human entry is permanently forbidden at the protocol level.
