SpeciedexMaps

The geospatial platform for mapping species distributions, occurrences, habitats, ecological regions, protected areas, environmental layers, provider coverage, uncertainty, and historical change across the Speciedex ecosystem.

SpeciedexMaps connects canonical taxonomic identities and species-chain records with geographic evidence. It provides map views for range records, occurrence points, habitats, migration, marine and terrestrial regions, conservation boundaries, environmental context, and historical distribution changes.

The platform is designed to preserve scientific uncertainty rather than hide it. Coordinates, range boundaries, source coverage, spatial precision, confidence, age, validation state, and conflicting provider records remain visible where permitted.

SpeciedexMaps may operate through SpeciedexWeb, SpeciedexExplorer, SpeciedexApp, SpeciedexAPI, or a local SpeciedexCore node. Map products remain tied to the underlying chain state, source records, licenses, and reproducible query definitions.

Permanent Safety Boundary

SpeciedexMaps does not provide human location history, person tracking, biometric mapping, genomic profiling, or Homo sapiens species records.

Sensitive species coordinates may be generalized, delayed, restricted, or withheld when disclosure could facilitate poaching, collection, harassment, trafficking, nesting-site disturbance, or habitat damage.
