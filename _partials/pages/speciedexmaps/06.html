Responsible Geography

Uncertainty, Privacy, and Sensitive Species

Coordinate Uncertainty

Preserve uncertainty radius, precision, georeferencing method, coordinate system, source precision, and later corrections.

Generalized Locations

Distinguish exact, rounded, centroid, regional, obscured, delayed, and withheld locations instead of presenting them as equivalent.

Sensitive Species Controls

Restrict coordinates and field details that could facilitate poaching, collection, trafficking, disturbance, or habitat damage.

Source-Specific Restrictions

Respect provider licenses, institutional restrictions, conservation policies, legal protections, and embargo periods.

No Human Tracking

The platform does not provide person search, human location history, biometric maps, human genomic layers, or population surveillance.

Contextual Human Data

Human activity may appear only where necessary as environmental pressure, conservation action, authorship, provenance, legal context, or interaction metadata linked to permitted non-human species.

Map Availability Does Not Authorize Field Access

A displayed location does not grant permission to trespass, collect specimens, disturb habitat, approach wildlife, enter protected areas, or violate local law.
