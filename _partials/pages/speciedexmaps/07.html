Validation and Provenance

Coordinate Validation

Check coordinate format, datum, bounds, precision, uncertainty, elevation, depth, and impossible values.

Range Plausibility

Compare records with known range, habitat, season, migration, barriers, and documented introductions.

Evidence Validation

Link spatial claims to media, specimens, surveys, sensors, publications, methods, hashes, and reviewer state.

Source Provenance

Preserve provider identity, original identifier, retrieval date, license, transformation history, and chain position.

Conflict Analysis

Expose contradictory coordinates, ranges, classifications, temporal claims, and provider interpretations.

Historical Revision

Preserve superseded points, corrected georeferences, revised range polygons, retractions, and changed uncertainty.

Restricted-State Validation

Confirm that sensitive locations remain generalized or withheld across maps, APIs, exports, caches, and shared links.

Chain Receipt

Resolve accepted geographic records to species chain, block or entry, validation result, object references, and later revisions.
