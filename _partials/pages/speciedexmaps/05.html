Search, Filtering, and Spatial Query

Taxonomic Search

Search species, synonyms, taxa, clades, ranks, authorities, identifiers, and historical classifications.

Spatial Search

Query bounding boxes, polygons, points, regions, watersheds, ecological zones, protected areas, elevation, or depth.

Temporal Filters

Limit observations, ranges, assessments, provider records, revisions, and map layers by date or chain state.

Evidence Filters

Filter by photograph, audio, video, specimen, sensor, literature, environmental DNA, or other evidence classes.

Provider and Provenance Filters

Select provider, institution, source, license, retrieval date, validator, transformation state, or confidence level.

Quality Filters

Locate stale, incomplete, uncertain, conflicted, generalized, duplicate, improbable, or weakly evidenced spatial records.
