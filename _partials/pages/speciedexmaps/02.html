Purpose

A Verifiable Geography Layer for Biodiversity

Species-Centered Geography

Connect maps directly to stable species and taxon identifiers rather than relying only on display names that may change through taxonomic revision.

Chain-Aware State

Reconstruct geographic views at a selected chain height, release, date, provider snapshot, or validation state.

Multiple Spatial Sources

Compare occurrence providers, institutional collections, range assessments, field projects, environmental layers, protected areas, and regional authorities.

Uncertainty-Preserving

Retain coordinate uncertainty, generalized locations, approximate ranges, disputed records, modeled areas, and source-specific precision.

Public and Scientific Use

Support education, conservation, field planning, museum research, ecological analysis, provider comparison, and reproducible publication.

Local and Remote Operation

Use a local SpeciedexCore node, institutional deployment, trusted gateway, or public web service according to privacy, verification, and availability needs.
