Bitcoin

An educational introduction to Bitcoin, Bitcoin Core, private-key ownership, network validation, mining, monetary policy, transaction privacy, Lightning, and the principles Speciedex draws from open decentralized infrastructure.

The Bitcoin page explains the technology and responsibilities behind Bitcoin rather than repeating the global site header. It is intended for readers who want to understand what Bitcoin is, how its network reaches agreement, why independent verification matters, and how users can reduce avoidable custody and security risks.

Bitcoin is an open peer-to-peer monetary network that allows people to hold and transfer value without depending upon one central bank, payment processor, government database, or private financial intermediary. Its rules are implemented in open-source software and enforced by independently operated nodes.

Speciedex supports Bitcoin as a resilient, borderless, censorship-resistant payment system and as an example of durable public infrastructure built from cryptography, transparent rules, distributed consensus, economic incentives, independent verification, and voluntary participation.

Bitcoin also requires care. Transactions are generally irreversible, private keys control funds, public blockchain data can be analyzed, software and custodians can fail, and legal or tax responsibilities vary by jurisdiction.
