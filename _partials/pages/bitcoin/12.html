Scam and Impersonation Awareness

Speciedex.org does not promise investment returns, guaranteed profits, mining income, token distributions, trading signals, recovery services, or doubled payments.

Speciedex representatives will not privately request that users transfer funds to unlock an account, verify a wallet, claim a prize, recover an investment, activate a token, or participate in a secret opportunity.

Verify website addresses, donation addresses, software downloads, release signatures, and official contact information before taking action.

Anyone claiming to represent Speciedex while requesting private keys, recovery phrases, remote access, gift cards, prepaid fees, or urgent cryptocurrency transfers should be treated as fraudulent.
