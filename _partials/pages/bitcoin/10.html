Project Infrastructure

Bitcoin and Speciedex

Donations

Speciedex may accept Bitcoin contributions to support software development, research, infrastructure, storage, documentation, hosting, archival work, and project maintenance.

Official donation information is published only through the Speciedex donation page and other verified project channels.

Open Infrastructure

Bitcoin's design influences the Speciedex commitment to independently verifiable data, open software, durable public infrastructure, transparent rules, and resistance to single points of failure.

Research

Speciedex may research distributed systems, timestamping, digital provenance, content addressing, archival verification, cryptographic signatures, decentralized identity, and durable scientific records.

No Token

Speciedex does not require a proprietary token, speculative asset, or separate project currency in order to function.

Where digital payments are appropriate, the project prefers established Bitcoin and Lightning infrastructure rather than creating an unnecessary token.
