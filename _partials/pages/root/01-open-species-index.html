Explore the Complete Speciedex Ecosystem

This root page is the primary orientation point for Speciedex.org. It presents Speciedex as a complete biodiversity infrastructure system, explains how its coordinated platforms fit together, and provides the principal routes into the project's scientific data, software, archives, research tools, public interfaces, and Bitcoin-native services.

Speciedex is the entire concept in one. It is supported by twelve specialized platforms: SpeciedexCore, SpeciedexExplorer, SpeciedexNet, SpeciedexWeb, SpeciedexTerminal, SpeciedexLibrary, SpeciedexArchives, SpeciedexGeneticBank, SpeciedexAnalysis, SpeciedexMaps, SpeciedexApp, and SpeciedexAPI. Each platform addresses a distinct operational need while remaining connected through shared identifiers, schemas, provenance records, validation rules, archival formats, and synchronization procedures.

Visitors can use this page to understand the purpose of the complete ecosystem before entering a specialized interface. Researchers can move toward comparative analysis, taxonomic records, scientific literature, maps, genetic references, provider provenance, and reproducible datasets. Developers and institutions can continue into APIs, terminal tools, core libraries, distributed mirrors, archives, applications, and machine-readable services. Educators, students, conservationists, governments, and the public can follow the same infrastructure through accessible web interfaces and documented open resources.

The sections below describe the relationship between all thirteen platforms, the common infrastructure they share, their uses in research and education, the project's Bitcoin-native monetary and verification layer, its open-source development model, and the complete directory of platform-specific pages. Together, they provide a comprehensive introduction to what Speciedex is, how it operates, who it serves, and where each part of the project can be explored in greater depth.
