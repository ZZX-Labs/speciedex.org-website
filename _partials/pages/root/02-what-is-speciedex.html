Open Biodiversity Infrastructure

What is Speciedex?

Speciedex is an open-source, Bitcoin-native biodiversity infrastructure project designed to catalogue, organize, reference, analyze, distribute, and preserve information relating to every known species on Earth. It combines modern taxonomy, scientific metadata, biological records, distributed systems, archival preservation, software tools, open documentation, and public educational resources within a unified digital ecosystem.

Speciedex is intended to serve researchers, taxonomists, conservationists, universities, museums, biological repositories, environmental organizations, software developers, educators, students, citizen scientists, and the general public. Its purpose is not merely to display species records, but to establish durable digital infrastructure for studying and preserving the biological knowledge upon which scientific research and conservation depend.

The complete Speciedex ecosystem contains thirteen coordinated platforms. Speciedex is the entire concept in one: the primary project, public species index, shared identity, and organizing system connecting every specialized component. It is supported by twelve interoperable platforms: SpeciedexCore, SpeciedexExplorer, SpeciedexNet, SpeciedexWeb, SpeciedexTerminal, SpeciedexLibrary, SpeciedexArchives, SpeciedexGeneticBank, SpeciedexAnalysis, SpeciedexMaps, SpeciedexApp, and SpeciedexAPI.

Together, these thirteen platforms provide database infrastructure, normalization and validation, web and terminal interfaces, network synchronization, scientific-library services, permanent archives, genetic-data references, analytical systems, geospatial tools, mobile and desktop access, machine-readable APIs, and public tools for exploring the relationships between organisms, classifications, habitats, genomes, scientific literature, geographic records, and conservation information.
