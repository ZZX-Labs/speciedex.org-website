Purpose

Preservation Beyond a Single Service

Independent Reconstruction

Preserve enough verified source material, schemas, manifests, software, and documentation to rebuild public Speciedex products without one surviving host.

Historical Reproducibility

Reconstruct what a release, provider import, taxonomy, policy, schema, or public interface contained at a selected time.

Provider Continuity

Retain source snapshots and documentation when APIs change, records disappear, providers close, or licenses are revised.

Format Migration

Support movement from older data formats, schemas, databases, compression methods, and storage media into maintained open formats.

Distributed Custody

Allow universities, museums, libraries, institutions, independent nodes, and public mirrors to preserve verified copies.

Forensic Review

Preserve logs, rejected records, repair reports, migration state, checksums, and historical manifests needed to explain failures.
