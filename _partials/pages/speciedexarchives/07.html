Durability

Storage, Replication, and Media Strategy

Multiple Independent Copies

Replicate archives across institutions, regions, jurisdictions, operators, storage systems, and offline media.

Open Archive Formats

Prefer documented, widely inspectable formats that do not require one proprietary application or service to restore.

Content Addressing

Identify large objects and archive members through stable cryptographic hashes independent of storage location.

Media Diversity

Support online mirrors, institutional storage, object stores, removable drives, optical media, tape, and controlled physical copies.

Retention Policies

Define what must remain permanent, what may be pruned, what requires legal review, and what belongs in restricted storage.

Periodic Verification

Recheck hashes, signatures, manifests, readability, dependency availability, and media health on a documented schedule.
