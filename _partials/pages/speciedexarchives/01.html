SpeciedexArchives

The long-term preservation system for taxonomic datasets, provider snapshots, scientific documents, historical classifications, metadata, media, schemas, reports, policies, manifests, licensing records, and recovery packages.

SpeciedexArchives preserves the materials required to reconstruct, verify, study, and migrate the Speciedex ecosystem after software, providers, institutions, hosting services, or network conditions change.

Archive packages are designed to remain inspectable outside a live Speciedex deployment. They retain documented formats, stable identifiers, immutable manifests, checksums, signatures, provenance, licensing, schema versions, release state, and recovery instructions.

The archive is not limited to current canonical data. Historical provider records, superseded classifications, rejected imports, prior schemas, old documentation, release candidates, archived software, reports, and earlier public states may all be preserved when they are required for reproducibility or forensic review.

Permanent Safety Boundary

SpeciedexArchives does not preserve Homo sapiens as a Speciedex species chain, biometric dataset, genomic profile, identity graph, or person-tracking archive.

Sensitive species locations, restricted collections, private credentials, wallet secrets, and prohibited personal data must remain excluded, encrypted, generalized, or access-controlled according to policy and law.

Preserve Releases

Store complete and partial public releases, provider snapshots, indexes, schemas, software, and documentation.

Verify Integrity

Use checksums, signatures, manifests, file inventories, timestamps, and reproducible generation metadata.

Retain Provenance

Preserve original source, retrieval date, license, transformation history, validation state, and chain relationships.

Support Recovery

Provide bootstrap packages, restoration instructions, migration tools, checkpoints, and disaster-recovery materials.
