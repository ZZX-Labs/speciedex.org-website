Quality

Data Quality and Provider Comparison

Completeness

Measure missing fields, incomplete lineage, absent evidence, unavailable media, missing vouchers, and weak documentation.

Freshness

Compare retrieval dates, update age, source releases, provider activity, stale claims, and delayed synchronization.

Duplication

Identify duplicate taxa, observations, specimens, accessions, media, references, and provider assertions.

Conflict

Detect contradictory names, ranks, ranges, traits, conservation states, identifiers, and source conclusions.

Plausibility

Flag impossible measurements, geographic outliers, inconsistent dates, unlikely habitats, or invalid lineage state.

Provenance Quality

Measure source traceability, license clarity, identifier stability, transformation depth, and unsupported derived values.

Provider Dependence

Quantify how much canonical coverage relies on one provider, one institution, one region, or one ingestion pathway.

Bounty Candidates

Convert verified data gaps, stale fields, missing evidence, translation needs, and unresolved records into reviewable tasks.
