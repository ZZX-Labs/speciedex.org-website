SpeciedexAnalysis

The scientific analysis platform for comparing taxonomic providers, measuring data quality, studying geographic and ecological patterns, reconstructing historical chain state, and producing reproducible biodiversity research.

SpeciedexAnalysis operates on verified species chains, normalized provider records, observations, specimens, geography, conservation assessments, genetic references, scientific literature, media, network state, and archival releases.

It is designed for taxonomists, ecologists, conservationists, biogeographers, institutions, developers, curators, educators, and researchers who need to move beyond simple lookup into systematic comparison, validation, statistical measurement, and reproducible interpretation.

Analyses remain bound to their source context. Provider identity, release, chain height, schema version, query parameters, filters, transformations, uncertainty, exclusions, and generated outputs are preserved so another user can reproduce or challenge the result.

Permanent Protocol Boundary

SpeciedexAnalysis permanently excludes Homo sapiens species records, biometric datasets, genomic profiles, identity graphs, person tracking, location histories, and human-population surveillance analysis.

Compare Providers

Measure overlap, conflict, missing fields, update age, taxonomy differences, and source dependence.

Analyze Quality

Detect stale, incomplete, duplicated, improbable, weakly evidenced, restricted, or unresolved records.

Study Change

Reconstruct how classifications, ranges, observations, threats, and evidence changed across releases and chain state.

Export Reproducibly

Preserve data selections, parameters, code, manifests, figures, tables, citations, and machine-readable results.
