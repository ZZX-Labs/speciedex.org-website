Distribution Change

Measure range contraction, expansion, introduction, extirpation, migration, seasonality, and observation change.

Sampling Effort

Separate biological patterns from provider density, geographic access, survey effort, collection bias, and missing coverage.

Habitat Relationships

Compare species with habitats, elevation, depth, climate, land cover, hydrology, vegetation, geology, and protected areas.

Interaction Networks

Measure connectedness, host breadth, dependency, ecological roles, co-occurrence, and documented interaction strength.

Threat and Protection

Compare threats, legal protection, protected-area coverage, habitat loss, recovery action, and evidence age.

Sensitive-Species Controls

Preserve generalized or withheld locations and prevent restricted coordinates from reappearing through analysis, exports, caches, or derived maps.
