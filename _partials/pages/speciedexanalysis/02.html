Purpose

A Reproducible Biodiversity Analysis Layer

Verified Inputs

Use chain-linked records, source assertions, provider snapshots, archival releases, and documented derived indexes instead of opaque flattened datasets.

Explicit Scientific State

Distinguish canonical summaries from disputed, provisional, superseded, rejected, restricted, or unresolved material.

Cross-Provider Comparison

Align records from multiple authorities without erasing original identifiers, licenses, retrieval dates, or conflicts.

Historical Reconstruction

Rebuild an analysis at a selected release, provider state, chain height, checkpoint, date, or schema version.

Independent Verification

Preserve enough source, query, code, manifest, and output information for another researcher to reproduce the result.

Human and Machine Use

Support interactive workspaces, notebooks, command-line pipelines, APIs, reports, dashboards, institutional systems, and automated validation.
