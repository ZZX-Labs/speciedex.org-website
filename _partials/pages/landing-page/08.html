Bitcoin and Verification Resources

Open protocols, reference software, payment infrastructure, technical standards, and timestamping tools relevant to the Bitcoin-native architecture of Speciedex.

These external resources document the systems from which Speciedex draws its monetary, verification, and distributed-infrastructure principles. They are provided as references for users who want to understand Bitcoin, operate validating software, study technical standards, or inspect open timestamping methods.
