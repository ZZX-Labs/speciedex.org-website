Speciedex is the complete concept in one. Twelve specialized platforms support that unified system: SpeciedexCore, SpeciedexExplorer, SpeciedexNet, SpeciedexWeb, SpeciedexTerminal, SpeciedexLibrary, SpeciedexArchives, SpeciedexGeneticBank, SpeciedexAnalysis, SpeciedexMaps, SpeciedexApp, and SpeciedexAPI. The sections below explain what each platform contributes, why the project exists, how Bitcoin informs its infrastructure, and how to begin using or supporting the work.

Use this page to identify the part of Speciedex that matches your purpose. Researchers can continue into taxonomy, literature, genetic references, maps, provenance, and comparative analysis. Developers can enter the core libraries, terminal, APIs, schemas, downloads, and source documentation. Institutions can review archives, mirrors, synchronization, preservation, partnerships, and public infrastructure. Educators, students, conservationists, governments, and the public can begin with accessible browsing, visual exploration, documentation, and learning resources.

The landing page is designed for first-time visitors and returning users who know what they want to accomplish but may not yet know which Speciedex platform, resource, or service is the correct starting point. It provides a concise route into the complete ecosystem without duplicating the global site header, the root-page overview, or the operational dashboard.
