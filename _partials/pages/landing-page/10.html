Contribute to the Speciedex Ecosystem

The long-term objective is not merely to build a large database. It is to establish durable, openly documented, independently verifiable infrastructure through which biodiversity knowledge can be studied, preserved, cited, distributed, and extended for generations.

Researchers, taxonomists, developers, librarians, archivists, museums, universities, governments, conservation organizations, educators, designers, translators, infrastructure operators, security reviewers, and volunteers are all relevant to the project’s success. Contributions should preserve scientific provenance, respect licensing and ethical constraints, remain reviewable, and improve the long-term public utility of the system.

Speciedex depends on work across science, software, documentation, infrastructure, design, preservation, translation, education, and public participation. Contributions can be small and specific or long-term and institutional: correcting a record, reviewing a provider, writing a guide, improving accessibility, testing a release, operating a mirror, integrating a collection, funding archival work, or developing an entire platform.
