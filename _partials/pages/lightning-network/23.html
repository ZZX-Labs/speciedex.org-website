Applications

Lightning Use Cases

Retail Payments

Merchants can receive rapid Bitcoin payments for products, services, admission, food, transportation, and other ordinary purchases.

Micropayments

Low-value payments can support articles, media, software functions, digital resources, API requests, and machine-to-machine services.

Donations

Lightning can support small, immediate contributions to creators, nonprofits, research projects, conservation work, and public infrastructure.

Streaming Payments

Applications may divide value into repeated small payments delivered over time as media, bandwidth, computation, or services are consumed.

Gaming

Games may use Lightning for player rewards, entry fees, digital purchases, tournament payouts, and real-time value transfer.

International Payments

Lightning can transmit bitcoin across borders without requiring correspondent banking relationships or conventional card networks.

Authentication

Lightning-related cryptographic authentication protocols can allow compatible wallets and applications to authenticate users without conventional passwords.

Automated Systems

Software agents and connected devices can create, request, send, and verify payments through programmatic interfaces.
