Lightning and Speciedex

Donations

Speciedex may accept Lightning contributions to support hosting, software, documentation, research, archival storage, conservation technology, and project maintenance.

Contributor Payments

Lightning may support small, rapid payments to contributors, translators, researchers, developers, photographers, and data-review participants.

Research Bounties

Public tasks may use Lightning for reproducible research, species-data corrections, metadata review, documentation, testing, and software-development bounties.

API Payments

Lightning may support machine-readable payments for high-volume services, specialized datasets, computation, archival access, or externally funded infrastructure.

Conservation Funding

Small direct payments may support verified field observations, habitat work, rescue operations, research expeditions, and local conservation initiatives.

Public Infrastructure

Lightning can provide a Bitcoin-native payment layer without requiring Speciedex to create a proprietary token, private blockchain, or speculative project currency.
