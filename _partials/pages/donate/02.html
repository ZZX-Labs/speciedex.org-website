Why Donate

Speciedex.org is developed as an independent technical, scientific, archival, and public-infrastructure project. Contributions help fund the systems and resources required to keep the project available, maintained, secure, documented, and actively developed.

Donations may reduce dependence on advertising, surveillance-based funding, closed data products, restrictive platforms, or technical decisions driven primarily by short-term commercial requirements.

General donations may be allocated among current project needs unless a separate written agreement establishes a restricted purpose.

What Donations May Support

  • Speciedex public index development
  • SpeciedexCore maintenance and provider ingestion
  • SpeciedexExplorer research and interface development
  • SpeciedexNet mirrors and distributed infrastructure
  • SpeciedexWeb, Terminal, Library, Archives, and API services
  • Database processing, validation, reconciliation, and exports
  • Public documentation, manuals, tutorials, and technical papers
  • Hosting, storage, backups, bandwidth, and monitoring
  • Security testing, maintenance, and incident response
  • Open-source releases, checksums, signatures, and archives
  • Research, testing, accessibility, and preservation work
  • Contributor, contractor, or specialist support where approved
