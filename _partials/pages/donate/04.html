Transaction Safety

Verify Before Authorizing a Bitcoin Donation

Bitcoin transactions are irreversible and must be checked carefully before broadcast.

Important

Always verify that the Bitcoin address displayed in your wallet exactly matches the address published on Speciedex.org before authorizing a transaction.

Speciedex.org cannot recover funds sent to an incorrect address, unsupported network, incompatible asset, altered QR code, compromised clipboard, or unintended recipient.
