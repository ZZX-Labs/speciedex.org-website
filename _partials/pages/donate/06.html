Donation Transparency

Donations support the Speciedex project generally unless a separate written agreement identifies a specific restricted purpose, reporting arrangement, or institutional obligation.

Project needs may change over time. General donations may therefore be used where they are most useful across development, research, infrastructure, documentation, security, preservation, publishing, and related project expenses.

Receiving a donation does not create an ownership interest, partnership, employment relationship, service obligation, advisory relationship, fiduciary duty, endorsement, or entitlement to project control.

No Purchase or Investment

A donation is not a purchase of goods or services, an investment contract, a security, a loan, an equity contribution, a deposit, or a promise of financial return.

Donations do not guarantee access to unreleased software, private infrastructure, internal data, project governance, support services, consulting, influence over scientific decisions, or future products.

Public acknowledgment of a donor, when provided, does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, partnership, or approval of the donor’s products, conduct, political views, or other activities.
