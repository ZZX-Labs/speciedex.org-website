Support Speciedex.org

A public donation page for supporting the continued development, research, documentation, infrastructure, security, preservation, and availability of the Speciedex ecosystem.

The Donate page explains how financial support may be provided, what project work donations may support, which payment method is currently published, and which legal and technical responsibilities remain with the donor.

Speciedex is developed as an independent technical and research project. Donations may help sustain software development, provider integration, database processing, public services, archives, documentation, testing, security work, hosting, bandwidth, backups, publishing, and long-term preservation.

A donation does not purchase ownership, governance authority, privileged access, unreleased software, private data, consulting, support, employment, partnership status, or a financial return. Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise, donations support the project generally.

Bitcoin transactions are irreversible. Donors must independently verify the complete destination address, selected network, transaction amount, wallet behavior, and any tax, accounting, legal, or reporting obligations before sending funds.
