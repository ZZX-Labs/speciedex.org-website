Trust Boundaries

Security and Constitutional Rules

Permanent Human Exclusion

Homo sapiens is forbidden from being entered as a species blockchain, taxon record, observation target, specimen subject, bounty subject, biometric subject, genomic profile, or derived identity profile.

The exclusion is constitutional to the protocol and is not an administrative preference that an operator may disable.

Boundary Enforcement

Validation rejects direct human records, aliases, disguised taxonomic entries, biometric datasets, genomic profiles, facial or gait records, and records whose practical purpose is tracking or profiling people.

Incidental references to human activity may appear only as necessary environmental context, provenance, authorship, conservation pressure, legal status, or interaction metadata attached to permitted non-human records.

Least Authority

Peer access, API keys, signing keys, reviewer roles, wallet controls, publication rights, storage access, and administrative services remain separated.

Safe Failure

Invalid updates, corrupt objects, incomplete synchronization, incompatible schemas, failed migrations, and suspect imports remain quarantined and cannot replace verified local state.

Authenticated Administration

Administrative APIs and local services require explicit authorization, protected credentials, bounded permissions, logging, and secure transport.

Private-Data Minimization

Core storage and public interfaces avoid unnecessary personal data, redact sensitive locations, and separate contributor identity from public chain state.
