Capabilities

Core Services

Chain Engine

Creates, validates, stores, indexes, traverses, checkpoints, and repairs the independent blockchain assigned to each permitted species.

Consensus and Policy

Applies protocol rules, validator policy, schema versions, review thresholds, conflict handling, and permanent human-exclusion enforcement.

Provider Ingestion

Imports approved APIs, archives, feeds, institutional exports, field packages, and manually reviewed submissions.

Reconciliation

Links names, records, identifiers, taxa, places, references, media, and observations without erasing source disagreement.

Search and Indexing

Builds full-text, taxonomic, geographic, temporal, identifier, trait, relationship, provider, chain-state, and provenance indexes.

P2P Synchronization

Discovers peers, exchanges inventories, requests missing blocks and objects, verifies received content, and resumes interrupted transfers.

API and Feed

Exposes read, query, validation, submission, status, streaming, replay, administrative, and export interfaces for local and remote clients.

Bitcoin Integration

Supports bounty definitions, invoices, payout records, Lightning settlement, on-chain settlement, fee accounting, and auditable reward state.

Archive Management

Creates and verifies snapshots, release packages, removable-media archives, provider bundles, recovery indexes, and long-term manifests.

Diagnostics and Repair

Detects corruption, missing objects, chain gaps, incompatible schemas, failed migrations, stale indexes, and peer inconsistencies.
