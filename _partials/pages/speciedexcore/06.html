Real-Time Feed

Data Input/Output Validation Feed

Ticker-Like Stream

SpeciedexCore exposes a continuous event stream showing submissions, validation stages, accepted blocks, rejected records, conflicts, superseding corrections, peer propagation, bounty state, and chain health.

Machine Interfaces

Clients may consume the feed through documented streaming HTTP, WebSocket, Server-Sent Events, JSON Lines, local sockets, or message-bus adapters.

Human Interfaces

Operators can watch readable status lines, dashboards, timelines, filters, severity levels, species channels, provider channels, and validator explanations.

Deterministic Replay

Feed events include stable identifiers and sequence positions so analyses, dashboards, audits, and incident reviews can be reproduced from a checkpoint.

Backpressure and Retention

Consumers can resume from known positions, request bounded history, and avoid losing state when temporarily disconnected or slower than the live feed.

Redaction and Access Control

Public feeds omit sensitive locations, private participant details, administrative secrets, and restricted records while preserving auditable status.
