SpeciedexNet

The decentralized peer-to-peer networking and replication layer that discovers, transports, verifies, synchronizes, relays, mirrors, and preserves the independent blockchain maintained for every permitted species.

SpeciedexNet connects independently operated SpeciedexCore nodes without requiring one permanent central data host. Nodes exchange verified chain blocks, shared objects, manifests, indexes, releases, peer information, and service metadata according to documented protocol rules.

Different operators may serve different roles. Full-weight nodes preserve the complete corpus, archive nodes retain historical material, regional and selective nodes prioritize defined subsets, gateway nodes serve clients, relay nodes improve reachability, media nodes store licensed objects, and field nodes synchronize after disconnected operation.

Every received object is independently checked before it can replace or extend verified local state. Peer identity, transport security, content hashes, signatures, ancestry, schemas, dependencies, and policy rules remain distinct verification layers.

A full-weight server node preserves a verified one-to-one, one-hundred-percent copy of the complete public Speciedex corpus. Selective nodes may hold less data while retaining cryptographic verification paths for the content they follow.

Permanent Network Boundary

Every compliant node permanently rejects Homo sapiens chains, human biometric records, human genomic profiles, and data whose practical purpose is tracking or profiling people.

This is a constitutional protocol rule, not an operator setting.
