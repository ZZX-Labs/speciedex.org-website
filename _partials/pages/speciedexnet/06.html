Verification

Consensus and Scientific State

Protocol Consensus

Consensus establishes valid ordering, signatures, schemas, policy compliance, chain ancestry, dependency availability, and canonical network state.

Scientific Disagreement

Consensus does not pretend that contested taxonomy, uncertain observations, or competing classifications have become scientifically certain. Disagreement remains attributable data.

Corrections Without Erasure

Later accepted records can supersede, qualify, dispute, or retract earlier assertions, but the historical sequence remains verifiable.

Canonical State

Nodes derive current canonical summaries from verified chain history while preserving the exact source assertions and review states used to reach them.

Conflict Visibility

Conflicting providers, reviewers, classifications, locations, and evidence remain visible for comparison instead of being silently flattened.

Human Exclusion

All compliant nodes enforce the permanent rejection of Homo sapiens chains, biometric datasets, genomic profiles, and human-targeted records.
