Network Model

A Peer-to-Peer Network of Species Chains

Decentralized Replication

Independent nodes exchange verified blocks, objects, manifests, indexes, releases, and peer information without requiring one permanent central host.

Operators choose their own infrastructure, jurisdictions, access policies, and service roles while following shared validation rules.

Distributed Responsibility

No single operator must carry every public function. Full-weight, archival, regional, gateway, relay, media, field, and selective nodes cooperate while remaining independently administered.

Species-Scoped Synchronization

Nodes may request and verify one species chain, selected taxonomic branches, regional packages, recent updates, provider subsets, media classes, or the complete corpus.

Complete Mirrors

A full-weight server node stores a one-to-one, one-hundred-percent verified copy of the complete Speciedex network dataset.

It can rebuild indexes and public services independently from verified chains, shared objects, manifests, and protocol rules.

Content-Addressed Objects

Large evidence, media, source packages, maps, documents, and research objects are identified by cryptographic content hashes and may be replicated separately from compact chain entries.

Service Independence

Search, APIs, web access, media delivery, archives, feeds, and peer relay can be provided by different nodes without assigning one operator authority over the scientific corpus.
