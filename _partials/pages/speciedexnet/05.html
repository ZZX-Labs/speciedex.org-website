Data Exchange

Synchronization Protocol

Inventory

Peers compare chain tips, checkpoints, object sets, schema support, releases, subscriptions, and available services.

Negotiate

Nodes agree on compatible protocol versions, encodings, compression, object classes, limits, and requested synchronization scope.

Request

A node requests missing headers, blocks, entries, objects, manifests, indexes, checkpoints, or signed release packages.

Transfer

Content is sent in bounded, resumable chunks with stable identifiers, sequence information, and integrity checks.

Verify

Every received item is checked for identity, ancestry, signatures, schema compatibility, policy compliance, and required dependencies.

Stage

Unverified, incomplete, incompatible, or suspicious data remains isolated from active indexes and public services.

Commit

Verified updates are atomically committed to local storage and made available to indexes, APIs, feeds, and downstream peers.

Announce

Nodes announce accepted chain tips and available objects without requiring other peers to trust the announcing operator.

Replay

Peers can resume from checkpoints or event positions and reproduce synchronization behavior for audits and incident review.

Prune or Retain

Nodes apply documented retention profiles while preserving the verification metadata required by their selected role.
