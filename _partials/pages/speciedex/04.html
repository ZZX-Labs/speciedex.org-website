Data Lifecycle

Submission, Validation, and Consensus

Submit

Users, institutions, software clients, field devices, and approved provider adapters create signed submissions using documented schemas.

Authenticate

The network verifies submitter signatures, permissions, identities or roles where required, and the integrity of referenced payloads.

Validate

The protocol performs structural, taxonomic, provenance, integrity, duplication, evidence, geographic, licensing, policy, and human-exclusion checks.

Review

Automated validation may be supplemented by peer review, expert review, source comparison, challenge periods, and conflict resolution.

Commit

Accepted records become ordered blocks or entries in the appropriate species chain and propagate through SpeciedexNet.

Replicate

Full-weight, archival, and service nodes synchronize accepted chain state, manifests, indexes, and shared reference objects.

Revise

Corrections do not silently erase history. Later accepted records supersede, qualify, dispute, or retract earlier assertions while retaining the audit trail.

Verify

Any node can verify hashes, signatures, ancestry, schema compatibility, source lineage, validation status, and current canonical state.
