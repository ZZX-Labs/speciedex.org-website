Decentralized Biodiversity Data Protocol

Speciedex

An open biodiversity protocol, dataset, and distributed public infrastructure for collecting, validating, preserving, studying, and serving the documented record of non-human life.

The Speciedex page defines the protocol itself. It explains how species-specific blockchains, validation rules, Bitcoin-native incentives, distributed nodes, public interfaces, archival systems, and human-exclusion safeguards fit together as one ecosystem.

Every permitted species is assigned a singular append-only chain. That chain stores the ordered history of signed submissions, evidence, revisions, disputes, validation outcomes, retractions, superseding assertions, and accepted scientific records associated with that species.

The complete Speciedex is the verified union of all permitted species chains, taxonomic indexes, shared reference objects, manifests, source records, schemas, protocol metadata, and reproducible public releases.

Bitcoin is used for monetary compensation, including validated contributions, data bounties, research work, archival support, contributor payments, and approved institutional programs. Speciedex has no proprietary speculative token.

Permanent Protocol Boundary

Homo sapiens is permanently forbidden from entry into the Speciedex system by protocol design.

The network is for the documented record of non-human life. It must not become a system for human biometric indexing, population tracking, genomic profiling, surveillance, or identity correlation.

Species-Specific Chains

Preserve the ordered scientific and evidentiary history of each permitted species independently.

Shared Protocol Rules

Apply common schemas, validation, cryptographic verification, peer discovery, transport, and archival procedures.

Bitcoin Incentives

Compensate accepted work through established Bitcoin and Lightning infrastructure without introducing a native token.

Independent Verification

Allow nodes to verify signatures, ancestry, schemas, provenance, chain state, and release integrity independently.
