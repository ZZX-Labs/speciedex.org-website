Media

Photographs, illustrations, audio, video, scans, models, maps, captions, attribution, licensing, and checksums.

Conservation

Assessments, population trends, legal protections, recovery actions, threats, protected areas, and sensitive-data controls.

Research

Publications, datasets, field reports, citations, claims, methods, code, supplementary material, and reproducible analytical links.

Geography

Ranges, occurrences, habitats, ecological regions, uncertainty, coordinate precision, historical change, and provider coverage.

Predation, symbiosis, parasitism, pollination, host association, competition, hybridization, and other biological relationships.

Protocol History

Submission signatures, validation reports, challenges, reviewer actions, revisions, retractions, supersession, and final chain state.
