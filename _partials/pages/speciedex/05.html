Bitcoin-Native Incentives

Data Bounties

Published tasks define required evidence, geographic scope, validation standard, deadline, reward, funding source, and dispute terms.

Validated Contributions

Eligible users may receive Bitcoin for accepted observations, digitization, classification, source recovery, translation, media, or field research.

Research Grants

Larger programs may fund institutional research, expeditions, digitization, software, archive recovery, or conservation work.

Layer One

On-chain Bitcoin may be used for larger awards, grants, escrowed programs, institutional work, treasury movements, and durable settlement.

Lightning Network

Lightning may be used for smaller rewards, rapid payouts, recurring micro-bounties, API metering, and low-cost international settlement.

Escrow and Milestones

Multisignature and smart-contract policies may coordinate staged funding, review checkpoints, disputes, and release conditions.

No Native Token

Speciedex does not require an altcoin, speculative token, or replacement monetary system. Bitcoin remains the monetary layer.

Transparent Terms

Eligibility, review state, funding source, fee policy, payout status, and settlement references remain auditable without publishing private participant data.
