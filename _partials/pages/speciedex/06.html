Homo sapiens Prohibition

Permanent Safety Boundary

Permanent Human Exclusion

Homo sapiens is forbidden from being entered as a species blockchain, taxon record, observation target, specimen subject, bounty subject, genetic profile, biometric subject, or derived identity profile.

The exclusion is constitutional to the protocol and is not an administrative preference that an operator may disable.

Boundary Enforcement

Validation rejects direct human records, aliases, disguised taxonomic entries, biometric datasets, genomic profiles, facial or gait records, identity graphs, and records whose practical purpose is tracking or profiling people.

Providers and adapters must apply exclusion checks before data can enter candidate chain state.

Permitted Context

Incidental references to human activity may appear only as environmental context, provenance, authorship, conservation pressure, legal status, cultural documentation, or interaction metadata attached to a permitted non-human species record.

Such context must remain limited to what is necessary to understand the non-human biodiversity record.

Safety Purpose

The boundary prevents Speciedex from becoming a human surveillance, biometric identification, population control, social scoring, or genomic profiling system.

It also protects the scientific scope of the network and reduces the risk of mission drift into human-data exploitation.
