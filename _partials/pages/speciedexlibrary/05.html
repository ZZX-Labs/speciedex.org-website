Scientific Linking

Taxon, Chain, and Evidence Relationships

Taxon Links

Connect works with accepted names, synonyms, historical names, ranks, clades, nomenclatural acts, and competing concepts.

Species-Chain Links

Connect a work to the chain entries, revisions, disputes, validations, and canonical states that cite or depend upon it.

Specimen Links

Connect publications with type material, vouchers, collections, repositories, accession identifiers, and examination records.

Dataset Links

Connect papers with deposited datasets, code, models, supplementary files, derived products, and reproducible workflows.

Observation Links

Relate publications to field observations, surveys, occurrence records, media, methods, and geographic evidence.

Genetic Links

Connect literature to permitted sequences, genes, markers, genomes, specimens, repositories, and accession records.

Conservation Links

Connect assessments, threats, population trends, recovery plans, legal protections, and supporting sources.

Citation Networks

Trace references, cited-by relationships, corrections, rebuttals, reviews, derivative datasets, and historical influence.
