SpeciedexLibrary

The structured scientific-library platform that connects species and taxonomic identities with papers, books, reports, field guides, citations, datasets, manuals, provider documentation, and legally accessible source materials.

SpeciedexLibrary organizes the literature and documentation that support the Speciedex species-chain corpus. It links scientific claims to publications, datasets, specimens, observations, provider records, taxonomic revisions, conservation assessments, methods, and archival evidence.

The Library does not replace publishers, journals, repositories, institutional archives, or established bibliographic databases. It provides a normalized interoperability and discovery layer that preserves identifiers, citations, provenance, licensing, version history, access locations, and relationships to Speciedex records.

A library record may describe a work even when the full text cannot legally be redistributed. Metadata, citations, identifiers, abstracts where permitted, access links, rights statements, checksums, and archived public versions remain distinct from restricted or copyrighted content.

Permanent Protocol Boundary

SpeciedexLibrary may preserve ordinary bibliographic information about human authors, editors, institutions, and historical context, but it must not create Homo sapiens species records, biometric profiles, genomic profiles, identity graphs, or person-tracking datasets.
