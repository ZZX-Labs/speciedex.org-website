Responsible Access

Rights, Access, and Preservation

Rights-Aware Records

Distinguish public-domain works, open licenses, repository copies, author manuscripts, restricted content, metadata-only records, and unavailable material.

No Unauthorized Redistribution

A bibliographic record or access link does not grant permission to redistribute copyrighted full text, images, scans, or supplementary files.

Access Locations

Record publisher pages, repositories, archives, libraries, institutional catalogs, lawful mirrors, and known offline holdings.

Checksums and Integrity

Publicly preservable files may retain cryptographic hashes, size, format, version, signature, and source metadata.

Long-Term Preservation

Coordinate with SpeciedexArchives for public-domain texts, open materials, metadata exports, manifests, documentation, and recovery packages.

Takedowns and Corrections

Preserve transparent metadata history while responding to lawful takedown requests, corrected rights information, broken links, and record errors.

Metadata Is Not Full-Text Permission

SpeciedexLibrary may describe, cite, and link to a work without possessing or granting redistribution rights to the complete work.
