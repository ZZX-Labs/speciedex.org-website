Research Workflows

Citation, Export, and Reproducible Research

Citation Builder

Generate citations from normalized metadata while preserving edition, page, identifier, access date, and source details.

Bibliography Export

Export permitted records as BibTeX, CSL JSON, RIS, JSON, CSV, TSV, text, or documented library packages.

Stable Links

Link to a current record, historical version, provider record, archived copy, selected citation, or chain relationship.

Research Notebooks

Connect reading lists, annotations, queries, taxa, chain states, datasets, exports, and analytical results.

Claim-Level Citations

Cite the exact source and page, figure, table, section, dataset row, specimen, or chain entry supporting an assertion.

Verification Metadata

Include record version, source provider, retrieval date, checksum, access state, license, and validation status.

Collection Manifests

Package selected metadata, identifiers, checksums, rights, relationships, and export definitions into reproducible manifests.

Institutional Interchange

Exchange normalized records with catalogs, repositories, reference managers, archives, APIs, and research systems.
