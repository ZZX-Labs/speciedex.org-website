Purpose

A Scientific Library Bound to Species Records

Literature Discovery

Find publications and documents relevant to a species, taxon, specimen, region, trait, provider, conservation issue, genetic record, or historical classification.

Evidence Context

Show which publications support, dispute, revise, or contextualize a chain assertion and which exact record relationships created that connection.

Normalized Metadata

Represent works through consistent fields while preserving original provider identifiers, citation forms, editions, translations, and source-specific metadata.

Source Independence

Maintain links to multiple publishers, repositories, archives, catalogs, and provider records without treating one external service as permanently authoritative.

Historical Continuity

Preserve superseded editions, earlier classifications, withdrawn papers, corrected records, errata, retractions, and changing access locations.

Public and Institutional Use

Support researchers, students, educators, museums, libraries, archives, conservation groups, developers, field teams, and public readers.
