Catalog Model

Bibliographic Records and Persistent Identifiers

Work Identity

Distinguish conceptual works from editions, translations, revisions, printings, chapters, articles, supplements, and archived manifestations.

Persistent Identifiers

Preserve DOI, ISBN, ISSN, Handle, ARK, accession, repository, catalog, provider, and local Speciedex identifiers where available.

Authorship and Contribution

Record authors, editors, translators, illustrators, institutions, data curators, reviewers, and other credited roles.

Publication Details

Preserve title, venue, publisher, volume, issue, pages, date, edition, language, series, and publication status.

Version Relationships

Link preprints, accepted manuscripts, published versions, corrections, retractions, supplements, datasets, and later editions.

Provider Provenance

Retain original source, retrieval date, source identifier, transformation history, license, validation state, and later updates.
