Mission Statement

This statement describes the enduring purpose of Speciedex rather than any single release, interface, provider, or development milestone.

The mission of Speciedex is to build and maintain a permanent, open, interoperable, independently verifiable, and publicly useful digital index of Earth’s biodiversity.

Speciedex brings together taxonomy, provider provenance, scientific literature, geographic distribution, conservation information, ecological relationships, genetic references, media, specimen records, observations, and historical classifications within one transparent framework.

The project is designed to make biodiversity knowledge easier to discover, compare, verify, preserve, exchange, cite, and reuse while respecting scientific attribution, licensing, privacy, conservation risk, cultural sensitivity, data sovereignty, and unresolved uncertainty.

The mission also requires that public software, schemas, archives, releases, and documentation remain portable and independently operable so the system can survive changes in institutions, funding, technology, and individual maintainers.
