The Mission of Speciedex

A complete statement of the problem Speciedex addresses, the public infrastructure it is building, the responsibilities it accepts, and the conditions by which its success should be judged.

The Mission page defines what Speciedex is obligated to accomplish. It does not repeat the global header, the landing page, the root overview, or the home dashboard. Instead, it explains why the project exists, which failures in today’s biodiversity-information environment it is designed to address, and what a responsible, durable response must include.

Speciedex exists to organize, verify, preserve, connect, and freely distribute knowledge about life on Earth through open science, open software, transparent provenance, interoperable standards, independently verifiable releases, and resilient public infrastructure.

The mission is broader than creating a large database. It includes retaining source attribution, representing scientific disagreement, preserving historical classifications, protecting sensitive information, supporting independent mirrors and archives, publishing machine-readable interfaces, and making biodiversity knowledge useful to researchers, institutions, educators, conservationists, developers, governments, and the public.

The sections below define the mission statement, the underlying problem, the project’s response, its objectives and commitments, the communities it serves, its Bitcoin-native principles, the measures of success, the limits it accepts, and the ways people and institutions can advance the work.

Organize

Connect fragmented taxonomic, scientific, geographic, conservation, genetic, specimen, media, and archival records.

Verify

Preserve sources, authorities, revision history, validation states, checksums, signatures, manifests, and reproducible processing information.

Preserve

Publish portable datasets, historical snapshots, open formats, recovery procedures, and independently maintainable mirrors.

Distribute

Provide public browsing, downloads, APIs, terminal tools, applications, archives, maps, analysis, and offline access.
