What Speciedex Is Not

Defining the mission also requires defining its limits. These boundaries prevent the project from claiming authority it does not possess or publishing information without sufficient scientific and ethical care.

Not a Replacement for Scientific Authority

Speciedex records and connects scientific claims but does not replace peer review, taxonomic expertise, field research, museums, journals, or recognized authorities.

Not a Single Unchanging Taxonomy

Classification evolves. The project should preserve competing systems, historical context, uncertainty, and revisions rather than conceal disagreement.

Not an Unrestricted Location Directory

Sensitive locations should not be published without considering conservation threats, law, research ethics, community interests, and exploitation risk.

Not Dependent on One Institution

The architecture should permit preservation and continued operation when individual organizations, services, or maintainers are unavailable.

Not a Claim of Ownership Over Source Data

Imported records remain connected to their providers, creators, institutions, licenses, and applicable restrictions.

Not a Substitute for Expert Review

Automated reconciliation, machine learning, and community contribution cannot replace qualified scientific evaluation where expert judgment is required.
